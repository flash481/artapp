# Research an art domain

**Preferred worker: Luna Max** (`gpt-6-luna` at max reasoning when selectable). Prepare a compact report for Sol's curriculum decisions, not a standalone survey course. This follows the project's objective to lower aggregate token/compute cost by routing high-volume work to capable Luna workers and reserving Sol for high-leverage reasoning; it is not a measured price claim. The identifier is a task/agent choice, not repository routing configuration.

Start from relevant book analyses and targeted source checks. Add reliable external sources when needed to resolve a gap or verify a claim; favor primary or authoritative material for technical, historical, or changing claims. Keep source titles, page references or links, and uncertainty with each important finding.

Define the topic and question narrowly. Summarize useful principles, teaching approaches, exercises, prerequisites, common learner difficulties, and connections to other fundamentals. Distinguish source consensus from disagreements and your own synthesis. End with what the curriculum designer needs to decide and what evidence remains missing.

Keep the report concise and private in `sources/research/`. Do not produce lesson sequences or resolve major pedagogy decisions; those belong to curriculum synthesis in `docs/CURRICULUM.md` and the `synthesizing-art-curriculum` skill.
