# Synthesize the curriculum

**Preferred lead: Sol High** (`gpt-6-sol` at high reasoning when selectable). This work needs global pedagogical judgment. Keep high-volume source processing and targeted evidence checks with capable Luna workers to lower aggregate token/compute cost, reserving Sol for this high-leverage synthesis; this is a project objective, not a measured price claim. Use Luna Max for targeted evidence checks or mechanical coverage summaries, not for the core architecture. These identifiers express task/agent choices only; do not invent repository routing configuration.

## Phase gate

Create the complete spiral course only in **Phase 2**, after the learner has added source books and the corresponding analyses are available. Phase 1 may contain only clearly labeled development fixtures. Do not begin a full course from ungrounded assumptions or organize it as one block per book.

## Synthesis decisions

Use concise structured analyses and domain reports, with page-level provenance. Merge overlapping concepts; distinguish consensus, complementary approaches, disagreements, and dated or weak material. If a decision turns on missing evidence, request a targeted Luna re-read and retain the source reference rather than loading full books into Sol context.

Design one integrated course, usually about 60–80 lessons (50–100 when justified). Establish learning outcomes, concept relationships, prerequisites, and lesson objectives for Luna to author. Let fundamentals recur across contexts: introduce, practise, revisit, combine, then support independent application. Plan spaced returns, varied subjects, and gradually reduced scaffolding instead of month-long topic blocks. Mix observational subjects, references, genuine master studies, learner-chosen subjects, and occasional stylized applications; stylized work must not displace early observational and structural training. Begin mainly with graphite and introduce charcoal where its qualities serve learning. Integrate art history through relevant works rather than a detached chronology; allow personal style to emerge through study and experimentation.

The handoff should give each lesson a concise objective, intended fundamentals/concepts, prerequisites, exercise intent, subject/scaffolding direction, and supporting source references. Keep learner-facing lesson prose and repetitive metadata with Luna. Use `docs/PEDAGOGY.md`, `docs/CURRICULUM.md`, `docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md`, and `docs/LESSON_SCHEMA.md` as design references. Maintain a coverage view of concepts introduced, revisited, combined, and independently applied so structural gaps are visible.
