# Review ArtApp lessons independently

## Purpose

Find concrete issues before a lesson enters production. Use a reviewer who did not author the lesson whenever available. Luna Max handles routine lesson and group QA; Sol 6.1 decides genuine pedagogical, source, architecture, or systemic questions.

## When to use

Use for one draft lesson or a bounded internal group, before integrating/publishing it. Use `reviewing-art-images` for visual approval and `reviewing-art-curriculum` for whole-course structural review.

## Required inputs

- Complete draft lesson record and internal authoring brief.
- Exact entry from `curriculum/CORE_DRAWING_MAP.md`.
- Relevant checked source packet and neighboring lesson summaries.
- `docs/templates/CORE_DRAWING_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md`, `docs/LESSON_PRODUCTION.md`, `docs/PEDAGOGY.md`, and `docs/LESSON_SCHEMA.md`.
- Relevant visual contracts and visual manifest entries.

## Required context

The 150-lesson map is frozen and is the authority for sequence, task, subject, prerequisites/returns, and scope. Current production records use the agreed stable IDs `core-001`–`core-150`; legacy pilot JSON and assets are prototypes only. Read lesson copy as a learner beside a sketchbook, especially on a phone. For lessons 01–10, source citations must be checked and traceable.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the map entry, draft, and evidence packet are present. If the map entry, source evidence, or complete draft is missing, return BLOCKER or NOT REVIEWED as appropriate; do not invent missing context. A draft-phase lesson may have a planned visual contract and fallback that are not yet generated: review lesson content provisionally, mark its visual/release status PENDING, and never claim release-ready until the real assets and provenance have passed independent review.
2. Compare title, lesson identity, objective, subject, activity, duration, prerequisites, concept stage, and later return with the map. Do not propose architecture edits for a local production defect.
3. Check that the objective is observable in the exercise; the learner actually draws; the task invites looking at a real subject/reference, comparison, diagnosis and correction; construction remains provisional. For every distinct mapped action, identify observable learner-completion evidence; a label is not evidence, and differently defined checks cannot substitute for each other (for example, edge-angle comparison versus two-landmark positional alignment). For each graphite effect, verify that the physical contact action is taught first and any applied mark has an observed purpose. Compare/correct wording must match the actual subject relationship and any paired image.
4. Check prerequisite capabilities, cognitive load, number of new ideas, neighboring recurrence, and whether known material is retrieved/transferred rather than unnecessarily retaught.
5. Check that `Have ready` names the physical subject/setup, reference route and usable fallback before the task. Confirm human lessons do not require another person and outdoor work has an ArtApp-owned fixed view.
6. Check graphite/tool readiness, warm-up scope, realism of drawing time, and total timing for setup, reading, looking, hesitation, drawing, comparison, correction, review, and a reserve. Identify any exercise that cannot fit without changing the map intent.
7. Check clarity, calm tone, accessible headings, short core copy, optional original Read More, accurate Sources & Further Reading, useful self-check, and the relationship of all requested visuals to the learner task.
8. Inspect citations against the evidence packet; check title, author, edition, exact page/section, and use. Mark uncertain source claims. Never treat a map source anchor as proof that a claim was checked.
9. Validate the record against the active lesson schema and inspect public/private field boundaries, stable ID, visual paths, inline provenance and separate asset records.
10. Return findings with severity and evidence. Do not rewrite the lesson unless directly assigned to fix it.

## Output contract

Choose findings from **BLOCKER**, **IMPORTANT**, **MINOR**, and **PASS**:

- **BLOCKER:** cannot proceed safely or faithfully (e.g., frozen-map task is contradicted, key source/rights fact is unresolved, required subject/fallback is unavailable, schema cannot represent the instruction, or a required technique is premature).
- **IMPORTANT:** materially weakens learning, access, timing, or course continuity and should be fixed before group acceptance.
- **MINOR:** local clarity, typo, metadata, or small timing adjustment; Luna author/integrator may resolve.
- **PASS:** no actionable issue after inspecting all required evidence.

Return lesson ID/status; severity-coded findings with exact field/step, evidence and smallest fix; a short pass over objective/exercise, access/skills, timing/correction, sources, schema/visuals and neighboring continuity; unresolved questions; reviewer's independence; and separate lesson-content and visual/release statuses. Mark **PASS** only for the scope actually reviewed. Do not report release-ready when fallback files, asset review, or provenance remain pending. Do not claim source checking that was not performed.

## Self-QA

- I reviewed the map and the actual lesson draft, not only a summary.
- I checked exercise alignment, prerequisite capabilities, learner load, and progression.
- I identified observable completion evidence for each distinct mapped action and kept different checks distinct.
- I checked physical graphite readiness, all subject routes, and a usable fallback.
- I examined whether the learner looks, compares, diagnoses and corrects.
- I accounted for real drawing and reserve time.
- I checked original optional teaching separately from evidence/citations.
- For lessons 01–10, I verified the actual source packet and precise locators.
- I recorded requested assets, schema fit, provenance, and public/private boundaries as passed, failed, or pending; planned assets are not treated as existing or approved.
- I used severity labels consistently and gave actionable evidence.

## Escalation

Report BLOCKER to Sol when faithful production appears to require changing architecture or when material pedagogy, prerequisite, source, rights, or schema decisions remain unresolved. Report IMPORTANT with an actionable local correction when the map remains clear. Ask for missing evidence rather than guessing. Sol decides whether an emerging issue is lesson-specific or systemic.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not change or recommend changing canonical order, title, module, objective, subject, or scope to make one draft easier.
- Do not infer that every prior lesson must be mastered; use actual capability links.
- Do not approve a lesson based on schema validity alone.
- Do not claim a source or asset was inspected unless you inspected it.
- Do not consider a supplied fallback optional when the map or production contract requires it.
- Do not accept a lesson that relies on untaught graphite effects or hides preparation until mid-exercise.
- Do not confuse Read More with sources or use decorative visuals to satisfy a count.
- Do not call an unreviewed draft approved, complete, or learner-tested.
