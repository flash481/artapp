# Core Drawing redesign — post-map red-team review

**Purpose:** record independent criticism of the first complete 100-entry map, concrete corrections, and the few issues left for human judgment. Three reviewers received the complete draft after its initial research and map were written: a pedagogy reviewer, a learner-experience reviewer, and a coverage/visual/scope reviewer. This report evaluates their findings; it is not a transcript of their reports.

## Existing 72-lesson audit carried into the redesign

The legacy map's strengths are its recognizable subjects, broad foundations, and later applications to people and sustained studies. The audit recommends preserving those capabilities and the enriched pilot rather than preserving its exact count or every lesson. Main weaknesses were excessive prerequisite chaining, negative space fading after its early lessons, convenient-object repeats without a changed transfer question, flower drift (dedicated anchors at legacy 04/16/34 and another optional use at 66), drapery appearing only once, adjacent extended projects at 59–60, charcoal/specialist creep, and mismatched source locators.

The revised map keeps the enriched first nine intact, brings negative shape back in the composition, rooms and people work, varies recurring objects by learning purpose, adds a modest support-based cloth cluster, separates its longer studies, removes charcoal from Core, and adds no flower-only lesson. Pilot 04 remains the one existing flower-or-leafy-stem measurement lesson; flower is optional in Lesson 82. Legacy lesson numbers are context only; the new 100-map is not a simple renumbering.

## Review result and remaining judgment

The map has been revised for all accepted findings. The only material architecture issue not resolved by editing is whether 13 human-subject entries are proportionate for Core. Workload, the limited landscape/nature allowance, human-reference rights/representation, and whether to replace or coexist with the legacy map are presented as human review choices in `CORE_DRAWING_REDESIGN.md`.

No finished lessons 10–100 or images were generated, and app implementation has not begun. The planning set is ready for curriculum review; production should wait for approval of the architecture and lesson map.
