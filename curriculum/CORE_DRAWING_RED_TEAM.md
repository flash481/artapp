# Core Drawing architecture — red-team reconciliation

**Scope:** independent reviews of the current 93-lesson plan, following the learner's instruction to treat pilot Lessons 01–09 as movable, mergeable material rather than protected sequence. This is a planning review only. No lesson JSON or visual asset was regenerated.

## Review rounds and findings

| Review | Concrete criticism | Decision and current correction |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot-wide opening review | The first nine touched line, contour, gaps, measurement, composition, value, construction and ellipses without enough nearby practice; later clusters risked duplicating rather than deepening them. | **Accepted.** The canonical map now keeps the baseline, moves line before contour, clusters silhouette/gaps/angles/overlap, and places measurement, composition, value, form and ellipses into their later focused clusters. |
| Opening learner red team | Every pilot concept should justify its early position against the whole curriculum; the opening needs real subject drawing and useful correction without becoming a concept sampler. | **Accepted.** Early studies are real-object based and build from mark control to contour/shape to an extended first application. The detached gesture preview was removed. |
| Beginner graphite audit | Point maintenance, grade choice, mark readiness and an oversized first extended setup were not explicit enough. | **Accepted.** Lesson 01 demonstrates/notes rotate and sharpen; 43 teaches common grade use with an HB-only route; 09 asks for one main object plus one overlapping secondary, with a third optional. |
| Beginner visual-plan audit | The L43 task asked for a five-step HB scale without a matching visual, and the L06 chair visual filled gaps with tone although its task only compares shapes. | **Accepted.** The L43 sampler now shows a five-box HB strip built from additional light layers. The L06 visual now outlines gaps without shading; the opening remains about shape evidence. |
| Beginner graphite audit | Hatching inside a negative-space gap could conflate tone with shape; a long gap before formal hatching and a weak L23→L25 return would make transfer uncertain. | **Accepted.** Lesson 05 is now strictly a shape/gap lesson. Lesson 23 teaches a short parallel patch inside a two-value thumbnail; 25 explicitly retrieves that simple value plan; 46–47 and 68 deepen hatch methods. |
| Beginner/source audit | Six supplemental graphite visuals had IDs but no production prompts; source locator WG18 pp. 43–44 did not support L02 line control. | **Accepted.** The manifest now has detailed prompts for grade sampler, broad layered tone, crosshatching, eraser lifting, blending comparison and page care; Lesson 51 explicitly shows light layering/highlight/hatch. The weak WG18 locator was removed. |
| Scope/subject/home review | Spoon and tin/spoon arrangements recurred by convenience; carton/kettle/cloth repeats needed a reason. | **Accepted in part.** Measurement 10 defaults to learner-selected personal objects; measurement group 16 is tin/carton/cloth; value hierarchy 50 is pear/jar/cloth. Carton/kettle/cloth remain in changed-light and extended value tasks because a controlled repeat makes the lighting comparison meaningful. |

| Stale-artifact audit | Old markdown, JSON maps and generator could still appear authoritative or recreate 72 lessons. | **Accepted.** Superseded maps/audits/generator and pilot plans are archived with explicit warnings; `docs/CURRICULUM.md` points to one canonical sequence; validator no longer imports the old lesson map. |

## Subject and access check

The current map's mode counts are 59 Life, 17 Either, 16 Reference and one Both intentionally used. Life rows specify a quick setup and supplied fallback. Human pose sets provide enough full-size references; no lesson requires another person. Flowers are not a strand: Lesson 05 is the one required plant-gap study, and flowers remain optional alternatives in measurement and the later leaf/branch overlap study. Landscape is limited to one short environmental application. Repeated household objects have a changed skill question, not a quota.

## Red-team disposition

Accepted findings were applied to the map and visual manifest rather than appended as caveats. The 13-lesson human module remains the only material architecture question for human review. The 93-lesson total is 5 Focused Studies, 77 Standard Lessons and 11 Extended Studies. The recalculated required target is 75 h 35 min, including 15 h 45 min of extended study.

No lesson 10+ production, image generation, app implementation or pilot JSON edits occurred. Approval of the map and visual specification remains the next human decision.
