# Core Drawing architecture — independent red-team and final review

**Status:** Review of the final 150-lesson planning architecture in [CORE_DRAWING_MAP.md](CORE_DRAWING_MAP.md) and [CORE_DRAWING_VISUAL_MANIFEST.md](CORE_DRAWING_VISUAL_MANIFEST.md). The learner approved the broad structure and requested this targeted final expansion. The architecture is content-complete after review. No lesson JSON, planned image, app code, or final visual asset was produced or changed in this pass; pilot Lessons 01–09 remain prototype/reference material.

## Review method

The map was reviewed independently for landscape false coverage, prerequisite order, whole-course false coverage, and specialist creep. A targeted landscape-domain note checked drawing evidence and its limits. Reviews were then checked against the actual updated map and manifest. This is an architectural audit, not an empirical evaluation of learner outcomes.

## Findings and decisions

| Review area | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape capability | Explicit practice covers view choice, crops, major shapes, simplification, space, terrain, trees, foliage/groups, rocks, values, atmosphere, sky/clouds, still/moving water, edge/detail choice, quick studies, changing conditions, correction and stopping. | Retained in early spiral encounters, Lessons 125–138, and later self-selected transfer. |
| Built/natural integration | First-draft routes allowed—but did not guarantee—a scene combining built and natural forms. | Lesson 127 requires a simple built boundary alongside terrain/vegetation; Lesson 138 requires one built form with surrounding natural forms. Both fixed-reference routes guarantee that composition. |
| Complete landscape at a shorter duration | A 90-minute capstone alone did not directly rehearse a complete 45–60-minute landscape. | Lesson 138 requires the complete readable scene, major spatial shapes, limited values and a comparison-led correction by minute 60; its remaining 30 minutes are selective refinement/review. |
| Water and reflection | Water could be treated as optional landscape decoration rather than a required foundational skill. | Dedicated Lesson 135 teaches surface plane and reflected relationships before ripple marks; 136 teaches observed flow. Both are required Core lessons. Later recurrence is invited only where water is visible. |
| Trees, foliage and groups | A token tree lesson would not be enough for observed structure or foliage economy. | L07 offers early silhouette/gaps; L117 transfers trunk-to-branch structure; L128 builds trunk-to-canopy; L129 groups foliage; L130 composes tree groups. |
| Outdoor depth and atmosphere | Foreground/background labels or lighter distance could become automatic rules. | L59 and L126 use observed cues; L133 compares the reference and rejects mechanical value/edge/detail changes. L138 combines them. |
| Quick sketches and changing conditions | A finished landscape alone would miss short field notation and changing subjects. | L137 distinguishes a 3-minute thumbnail, 5–10-minute note, and short study; establishes light/shadow early and simplifies/omits transient forms. |
| Access | Outdoor life drawing or weather could block the learner. | All outdoor lessons have ArtApp-owned fixed-view fallbacks. Outdoor access is encouraged and never required. |
| Prerequisite order | No hard sequence violation was found. An early tree-gap transfer precedes the general negative-space lesson by one slot, and the map initially had stale IDs/overbroad prerequisite lists. | Lesson 07 now identifies branch gaps as a preview of Lesson 08. Lesson 77 is placed at the end of Module 7. Stale cross-references and overstated prerequisites were corrected. Tree structure precedes foliage groups; broad value precedes atmosphere; calm water precedes moving water; the guided study precedes later independent work. |
| Whole-course balance | Adding landscape should not weaken existing strong strands or seed specialist creep. | No existing lesson was removed. Twenty lessons were added (six early, fourteen focused); Lesson 117 was sharpened in place. Specialist surf, waterfalls, detailed geology/botany, advanced architecture, color, and polished rendering remain deferred. |
| Subject-balance check | Landscape practice is substantial but remains one part of the course. | Net-new work is 885 minutes; landscape/nature practice including the broadened L117 is 930 minutes (13.3%). People-focused work remains 34 lessons / 1,635 minutes (23.4%). |
| Visual coverage | Every map lesson needs a matching planned visual record, but no final asset is authorized here. | Manifest headings/IDs run 01–150 and include original prompts and alt-text intent, including all landscape lessons. |
| Research evidence | Museum teaching examples and historic manuals are useful drawing evidence, but do not establish adult learning outcomes. | The private landscape note records source limitations; claims remain about planned practice, not proven outcomes. |

## Final duration audit

The final map contains **150 lessons** and **6,990 minutes (116 hours 30 minutes)**: 13 Focused Studies at 30 minutes, 126 Standard Lessons at 45 minutes, and 11 Extended Studies (four at 75 minutes and seven at 90 minutes). The Extended Studies are 14, 35, 50, 64, 76, 88, 105, 121, 138, 147, and 150.

The six new early landscape encounters add 210 minutes; the 14-lesson Landscape & Nature module adds 675 minutes. Thus the targeted expansion adds 885 minutes (14 hours 45 minutes) to the former 130-lesson architecture. Including the 45-minute tree-structure practice clarified at existing Lesson 117, landscape/nature-specific practice is 930 minutes (15 hours 30 minutes), or 13.3% of the final scheduled course time.

## Whole-course coverage conclusion

The independent whole-course audit found no material general-foundation topic that was merely named without drawing practice or left without a useful later transfer. Observation, graphite, measurement, composition, construction, perspective, value, figure/anatomy, portrait/likeness, hands/feet, edges/materials, organic forms, fabric, self-critique, and independent drawing remain intact. The landscape work is woven into silhouette, composition, form, space, value, edges, natural construction, and the final integration module, so it does not function as a detached specialist course.

The architecture still requires learner-pilot feedback on actual time, cognitive load, and accessibility. That is a production/experience check; it does not justify another content expansion in advance.

## Final disposition

**PASS — content-complete for Core Drawing.** The two material landscape gaps found in the first draft were closed and independently rechecked. Prerequisite corrections were incorporated. The anti-specialist-creep review found no lesson that needed to be cut and no further specialist additions that belong in Core. The canonical map and manifest are planning documents; production-specification, reference-rights/provenance, and pilot-progress migration decisions remain for the production phase.
