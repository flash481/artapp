# ArtApp Core Drawing — 130-lesson curriculum architecture

**Canonical proposed sequence; planning only; awaiting human review.** This replaces the earlier 93-lesson architecture. The sequence was rebuilt conceptually from a blank Lesson 01. Its count emerged from practice and transfer needs; it is not a quota. This map is not lesson copy, lesson JSON, app implementation, or approval to generate teaching images.

**Pilot status:** The published pilot JSON and assets for legacy Lessons 01–09 are prototypes/reference material only. Their IDs, order, subjects, exercises, visuals, and production investment do not constrain this course. Keep them unchanged as production references until the new architecture is approved; later reuse or replace individual ideas only on their teaching merit. Sequence numbers below do not reserve pilot content IDs.

## Design and use

- Start with a recognizable subject and drawing in Lesson 01. Teach only HB, comfortable hold, light first marks, gentle correction, and avoiding grooves in the paper.
- Use draw → observe → compare → diagnose → correct → redraw. Construction proposes; the observed subject/reference decides whether it fits.
- Graphite is a continuous strand: introduce a mark, immediately apply it, revisit, deepen, combine, then let the learner choose. Two-value grouping, side tone, even tone, basic directional hatching, layering, simple gradation, and preserving paper white are taught in Lessons 29–32, before full value study.
- Composition is explicitly taught in 22–33 and revisited in later arrangements, portraits, rooms, and extended studies; it is not forced into every technical exercise.
- Form progresses from observed solids to connected and modified volumes, then transfers to space, people, heads, hands, feet, natural forms, cloth, and independent studies.
- Life lessons have a low-friction setup and a named fixed-view reference fallback. Lesson 01 introduces photo use before the first fallback: choose a clear, uncropped view, note crop/viewpoint/light, and keep that view fixed while drawing. Lesson 121 develops a fuller photo audit. Human lessons use supplied references, self/mirror routes, or an optional willing model; no lesson requires another person.
- Focused Study = 30 minutes; Standard = 45; Extended = 75–90. Setup, drawing, comparison, correction, and review fit inside the time. Extended studies consolidate material already taught.
- HB completes every lesson. Extra grades and tools are optional. Core is graphite only; charcoal, color, detailed anatomy, specialist botanical work, and advanced landscape remain outside this course.
- Read More is optional original ArtApp teaching. Sources & Further Reading is separate and records real influence. Books, scans, extracts, analyses, and notes remain private under sources/.
- Each lesson entry gives practice, route/fallback, prerequisites and returns, Read More intent, and a visual ID. Module source anchors apply to the listed lessons. Detailed image prompts are in CORE_DRAWING_VISUAL_MANIFEST.md.

## Module 1 — Start looking and marking (01–05)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 10–69; WG18 pp. 15–37; P16 pp. 8–9, 21–23, 31–41; A11 Ch. 3.

### 01. Draw one real thing — look, mark, compare, change
**Practice (45 min):** Learner chooses a simple object, draws immediately with HB, compares one visible relation, and corrects it. Teach comfortable writing hold, light first marks, gentle erasing, and no paper grooves; label and keep this baseline for 119. If using a photo, check that the whole object/contact is clear, note crop, viewpoint and light, then keep that view fixed. Choose a reusable object/view or keep a quick setup photo so the baseline can be repeated. **Route:** Life, spoon/fruit/cup/package; fallback: fixed-view simple ArtApp reference. **Prereq/return:** none; revisit same view at 119, independent cycle at 130. **Read More:** first drawing as useful evidence, not a talent test. **Visual:** cd-core-l001-observation-cycle.

### 02. Keep the first marks light — pressure and paper
**Practice (30 min):** Calibrate light/medium/firm pressure on scrap, then make and correct a light block-in on a box or book; check the reverse for dents and place a spare sheet under the hand. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view photo, with pressure practice on the learner’s paper. **Prereq/return:** 01; return in 29–32, 44–46, 62–72, 84. **Read More:** pressure versus confidence and why reversible marks help. **Visual:** cd-core-l002-pressure-paper.

### 03. Choose point or side — angle, rotation, sharpening
**Practice (45 min):** Compare pointed contour and broad side marks at two pencil angles; rotate a rounded point and sharpen only when detail needs it; apply choices to fruit or a smooth object. HB only. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired close-up reference plus mark test. **Prereq/return:** 02; return in 30–32, 62–66, 84–90. **Read More:** pencil position changes contact; grip is functional, not fixed. **Visual:** cd-core-l003-point-side.

### 04. Search then select — line and correction
**Practice (45 min):** Make faint searching lines to locate a spoon/tool, compare, select a clearer contour, and correct the same drawing. Teach ordinary eraser and putty/kneaded eraser if available; single-eraser route uses a gentle scrap test. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view tool photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–03; return in 05, 44, 84, 91, 130. **Read More:** searching is evidence gathering; compare erasing, lifting, and leaving a guide. **Visual:** cd-core-l004-search-select.

### 05. Follow an edge — contour direction and turns
**Practice (45 min):** Draw several changing boundary directions on a shoe, shell, or tool; compare and correct two turns on the same subject before transferring one turn to a second object. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched side-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 01–04; return in 06–13, 34–47, 84–86. **Read More:** contour is selected; it may vanish at overlap or into value. **Visual:** cd-core-l005-contour-turns.

## Module 2 — Shape, gaps and overlap (06–13)

**Source anchors:** WG18 pp. 24–71; E12 PDF pp. 121–211; P16 pp. 26–41, 57–63; D85 pp. 128–147.

### 06. See the outside shape first
**Practice (30 min):** Draw a parcel, toy, or fruit as one envelope, add two large divisions, compare height/width/tilt, and correct the outside before parts. **Route:** Life; fallback: neutral single-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 05; return in 10–13, 34–47, 123. **Read More:** what silhouette can and cannot tell you. **Visual:** cd-core-l006-silhouette.

### 07. Draw the spaces around it
**Practice (45 min):** Use gaps around a chair or plant to check subject shape; compare gap angles/widths and correct the surrounding contour, without shading the gaps. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–06; return in 11, 15, 24, 120, 123. **Read More:** positive and negative shapes share an edge. **Visual:** cd-core-l007-negative-shapes.

### 08. Compare slants and alignments
**Practice (45 min):** Find three directions in scissors, a tool, or chair; compare tilt and one plumb alignment with a pencil; redraw the largest error. **Route:** Life; fallback: top-down arrangement photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–07; return in 16–18, 35–45, 48–60, 76–82. **Read More:** direction and alignment depend on viewpoint. **Visual:** cd-core-l008-angle-alignment.

### 09. Show which form overlaps
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a jar behind a carton; locate interrupted contours, add only useful faint through-lines, then correct the overlap and one tangency. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–08; return in 26, 41–42, 48–57, 102–107. **Read More:** T-junctions are evidence of occlusion, not a universal rule. **Visual:** cd-core-l009-overlap.

### 10. Divide a large shape into a few parts
**Practice (30 min):** Draw the envelope of a kettle, bottle, or packaged object, compare two or three large internal shapes, and revise one boundary without small detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: clear reference with visible divisions. **Prereq/return:** 06–09; return in 43–47, 92–99, 109–115. **Read More:** grouping; distinguish an edge from a value change. **Visual:** cd-core-l010-large-shapes.

### 11. Repeat and compare the gaps
**Practice (45 min):** Draw three books, cups, or shelf divisions; compare spaces between them as shapes and correct one interval on the same page. **Route:** Life; fallback: straight-on photo. **Prereq/return:** 07–10; return in 17–18, 24, 53–55, 123. **Read More:** repeated intervals help; equal real gaps may look unequal. **Visual:** cd-core-l011-repeated-gaps.

### 12. Read a contour through an interruption
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a handle behind a rim or a stem through leaves in visible sections; compare breaks with overlap and correct two interruptions before interior marks. **Route:** Life; fallback: high-contrast same-view reference. **Prereq/return:** 05, 07, 09–11; return in 41–42, 86, 113, 126. **Read More:** distinguish observed edge from imagined continuation. **Visual:** cd-core-l012-interrupted-contour.

### 13. Extended Study — two objects, one clear drawing
**Practice (75 min):** Set up two overlapping objects; plan placement, draw silhouette/gaps, pause to compare width, tilt, spacing, and overlap, then correct the largest issue. Keep the drawing. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied two-object photo. **Prereq/return:** 06–12; consolidates shape; baseline recheck at 119. **Read More:** select one useful correction over polishing every line. **Visual:** cd-core-l013-shape-study.

## Module 3 — Measure and begin figure observation (14–21)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 40–69; E12 PDF pp. 180–211; P16 pp. 26–29, 42–63; H09 pp. 3–28.

### 14. Choose a measuring unit
**Practice (45 min):** At a steady arm’s-length viewpoint, sight the object’s height as a temporary unit with a pencil/thumb mark; compare its width and one feature against that unit, transfer the ratio to a light block-in, and return to the same position between checks. **Route:** Life, bottle/book/appliance; fallback: same-view photo (compare visible ratios, not screen dimensions). **Prereq/return:** 06–13; return in 15–18, 20, 44–47, 75. **Read More:** comparative measurement is relational, not numeric perfection. **Visual:** cd-core-l014-unit-measure.

### 15. Check the whole — height, width and placement
**Practice (30 min):** Place height and width in a page frame, compare margins and widest point, correct envelope, then add two large shapes. **Route:** Life, box and round object; fallback: single-view image. **Prereq/return:** 14; return in 22–33, 119–130. **Read More:** page placement affects the read of an otherwise accurate object. **Visual:** cd-core-l015-whole-placement.

### 16. Check a long angle and alignment
**Practice (45 min):** Compare two major axes on an irregular object using a pencil sighting guide; check a plumb relation and redraw one direction. **Route:** Life, scissors/tool; fallback: matching photo. **Prereq/return:** 08, 14–15; return in 35–46, 50–55, 76–82. **Read More:** angle and length checks answer different questions. **Visual:** cd-core-l016-sighted-angles.

### 17. Compare repeated intervals
**Practice (45 min):** Measure three spaces in a row of jars, books, or shelf divisions; compare to the first interval and redraw the group without making every picture gap equal. **Route:** Life; fallback: straight-on reference. **Prereq/return:** 11, 14–16; return in 53–55, 125. **Read More:** interval is a shape and sometimes a depth clue. **Visual:** cd-core-l017-intervals.

### 18. Measure an irregular object
**Practice (45 min):** Compare envelope, axis, widest point, and two landmarks on a shoe, vegetable, or tool; use a light block-in and correct the whole before contour. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo with one clear axis. **Prereq/return:** 14–17; return in 109–115, 124. **Read More:** measurement supports irregular observation without regularizing it. **Visual:** cd-core-l018-irregular-measure.

### 19. Catch a pose in a few lines
**Practice (30 min):** Draw supplied short poses using an action line and two direction changes; compare pose direction and revise one line, without anatomy construction. **Route:** Reference, clothed adult poses; fallback: self/mirror pose or supplied sequence, never another person. **Prereq/return:** 08, 14–16; return in 20, 46, 72–83, 127, 130. **Read More:** gesture records action, not a stick-figure template. **Visual:** cd-core-l019-gesture.

### 20. Compare the pose — proportion and balance
**Practice (45 min):** Make two small full-figure block-ins from standing/seated poses; compare height/width, torso/leg relation, tilt, and ground contact; correct one proportion in each. No universal head-count template. **Route:** Reference; fallback: mirror/self or supplied poses. **Prereq/return:** 14–19; return in 46, 74–83, 127. **Read More:** proportion varies with pose and person. **Visual:** cd-core-l020-pose-proportion.

### 21. Study — measure a small arrangement
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a three-object still life; choose one unit, place the group, compare gaps/intervals, and correct the largest error before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied fixed-view arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 14–18; consolidates observation/measurement before composition. **Read More:** keep one comparison question active through a drawing. **Visual:** cd-core-l021-measured-study.

## Module 4 — Compose, simplify and use early tone (22–33)

**Source anchors:** GW14 pp. 14–70, 116–159; D85 pp. 148–197; P16 pp. 65–79, 81–95.

### 22. Frame the view — crop and scale
**Practice (30 min):** Make three crops of one arrangement, compare wide/close/off-centre readings, choose one, and draw a larger study preserving its boundaries. **Route:** Either life setup or photo; fallback: three matched crops. **Prereq/return:** 15, 21; return in 25–29, 33, 91, 121–123, 130. **Read More:** framing changes meaning without changing objects. **Visual:** cd-core-l022-crop-scale.

### 23. Place the subject on the page
**Practice (45 min):** Compare two placements of a familiar group; check margins, visual weight, and empty space; redraw the chosen placement at sketchbook size. **Route:** Either still life/photo; fallback: same-view image. **Prereq/return:** 15, 22; return in 33, 99, 122–130. **Read More:** centring is an option, not a rule. **Visual:** cd-core-l023-page-placement.

### 24. Balance positive and negative shapes
**Practice (45 min):** Compare gaps around three objects; adjust their arrangement if useful and compare revised gaps and silhouettes. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo with clear spaces. **Prereq/return:** 07, 22–23; return in 48–57, 102–106, 120. **Read More:** balance is a relationship, not a formula. **Visual:** cd-core-l024-space-balance.

### 25. Give the eye a place to begin
**Practice (45 min):** Make three thumbnails changing relative size, placement, or overlap; compare focal read and subordination. Required choices use placement/scale; value waits until 29. **Route:** Either, fruit and household object/photo; fallback: same arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 22–24; return in 32–33, 91, 98–99, 122. **Read More:** visual weight and hierarchy without a fixed recipe. **Visual:** cd-core-l025-focal-hierarchy.

### 26. Arrange depth with overlap and scale
**Practice (45 min):** Make two arrangements changing overlap; draw the clearer one and correct silhouette/ground contact before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of the objects. **Prereq/return:** 09, 22–25; return in 48–57, 74–83, 102–107. **Read More:** overlap and scale as observed depth and composition. **Visual:** cd-core-l026-depth-arrangement.

### 27. Find and fix a tangency
**Practice (45 min):** Diagnose near-touching contours as clear, ambiguous, or useful; change a life setup or test one thumbnail edit on a fixed image. **Route:** Either objects or photo; fallback: same-view image. **Prereq/return:** 09, 22–26; return in 91, 95, 122. **Read More:** tangency is a clarity question, not a ban on close edges. **Visual:** cd-core-l027-tangency.

### 28. Follow a direction through the frame
**Practice (45 min):** Find a path/rhythm in tools or a natural cluster; thumbnail how the eye travels, then draw major shapes without outlining every item. **Route:** Life; fallback: reference with visible rhythm. **Prereq/return:** 22–27; return in 33, 113–115, 122, 128. **Read More:** repeated angles, curves, and spacing can guide attention. **Visual:** cd-core-l028-rhythm.

### 29. Two values on a real object
**Practice (45 min):** Use one steady window or lamp from a single side; test a broad side tone and light parallel strokes, group a pear, cup, or carton into light and shadow, reserve paper white, compare, and correct the boundary. **Route:** Life under fixed light; fallback: fixed-light photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–03, 22–28; first tonal use, deepened in 30–32 and 61–72. **Read More:** simple reversible marks and two-value grouping. **Visual:** cd-core-l029-two-value.

### 30. Make an even tone and directional hatch
**Practice (45 min):** Build a short even band with light layers, then use one directional hatch on a visible plane/surface of a familiar object; compare pressure with repeated layers and avoid dents. **Route:** Life carton/book/fruit; fallback: simple two-value image. **Prereq/return:** 02–03, 29; return in 63–66, 84–90. **Read More:** stroke spacing and direction are choices. **Visual:** cd-core-l030-even-tone-hatch.

### 31. Try a simple gradation and preserve the light
**Practice (45 min):** Build a light-to-dark transition with pressure/layers, apply it to the observed turn of an egg or fruit, leave paper for the brightest light, and compare rather than fill every surface. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-light close-up. **Prereq/return:** 29–30; return in 35, 62–71, 114–118. **Read More:** distinguish observed gradation from a mechanically smooth strip. **Visual:** cd-core-l031-gradation.

### 32. Plan a focal value thumbnail
**Practice (45 min):** Reduce an arrangement to two/three value masses; thumbnail two dark placements, compare hierarchy, then apply the better plan to a short recognizable drawing. **Route:** Either life still life or photo; fallback: same-view crop. **Prereq/return:** 25, 29–31; deepen in 61–72, 97–99, 122. **Read More:** value grouping and focal emphasis. **Visual:** cd-core-l032-value-thumbnail.

### 33. Extended Study — compose a small still life
**Practice (75 min):** Arrange objects, compare three crops, choose a hierarchy, draw big shapes and a two/three-value plan, then pause for page/gap/focal review and one correction. **Route:** Life under simple light; fallback: supplied arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 22–32; consolidates composition and early graphite before form. **Read More:** omit a visible detail that weakens the read. **Visual:** cd-core-l033-composition-study.

## Module 5 — Construct observed form (34–47)

**Source anchors:** P16 pp. 57–63; D85 pp. 128–147; N99 pp. 21–66, 117–154; A11 Ch. 5.

### 34. A solid has planes
**Practice (45 min):** Propose front/side/top planes on a package; compare widths and visible edges; keep only useful guides. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 08, 14–16; return in 35–47, 51–60, 125. **Read More:** boxes model space but the observed view may resist ideal geometry. **Visual:** cd-core-l034-box-planes.

### 35. Find the axis through a rounded form
**Practice (30 min):** Mark long axis and cross-section of an egg or bottle; compare axis with silhouette and select contour lightly. **Route:** Life; fallback: close three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 14–16, 34; return in 36–38, 109–115, 76–82. **Read More:** axes are orientation checks, not required final marks. **Visual:** cd-core-l035-axis.

### 36. Read an ellipse — axis and opening
**Practice (45 min):** Compare a bowl rim from two eye heights; draw its axis and apparent opening, then compare to the actual rim rather than a template. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of same dish. **Prereq/return:** 34–35; return in 37–38, 58–59, 103–105. **Read More:** ellipse degree follows viewing orientation. **Visual:** cd-core-l036-ellipse.

### 37. Build a cylinder from its end planes
**Practice (45 min):** Draw axis, openings, and sides of a tube or bottle; compare taper, rim width, and axis; correct before darkening. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired height views. **Prereq/return:** 34–36; return in 38–47, 58–59, 79–82. **Read More:** aligned end planes help, but objects are not always true cylinders. **Visual:** cd-core-l037-cylinder.

### 38. Turn an egg and sphere
**Practice (45 min):** Use a few cross-contours to show turn; compare with silhouette and light, revise one line that makes the form read flat. **Route:** Life under ordinary light; fallback: two fixed views. **Prereq/return:** 35–37; return in 109–115, 92–97, 66–69. **Read More:** cross-contour is checked imagination, not decoration. **Visual:** cd-core-l038-round-volume.

### 39. Make a tapered form
**Practice (45 min):** Compare axis and changing width of funnel, carrot, or tapered container; build broad-to-narrow volume and correct silhouette/opening. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo along its axis. **Prereq/return:** 34–38; return in 40–43, 109–112. **Read More:** taper is a volume change, not an outline trick. **Visual:** cd-core-l039-taper.

### 40. Join and intersect simple volumes
**Practice (45 min):** Find two or three masses in a watering can, lamp, or stapler; mark axes/attachments, observe overlap, and correct one join. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 34–39; return in 41–47, 76–82, 100–108, 117–118. **Read More:** attachment, overlap, and merging are different relationships. **Visual:** cd-core-l040-joined-forms.

### 41. Draw through, then select what is visible
**Practice (45 min):** Use faint through-lines on a chair or table; compare hidden guides with actual overlap, strengthen visible edges and leave hidden structure pale/erased. **Route:** Either; fallback: three-quarter chair photo. **Prereq/return:** 09, 34, 37, 40; return in 48–57, 78–83, 84–86. **Read More:** hidden construction checks, not mandatory visible lines. **Visual:** cd-core-l041-draw-through.

### 42. Use a simple form without forcing it
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a cup, kettle, or appliance partly resembling a box/cylinder; mark where the model stops helping and adjust it to observed asymmetry. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched photo. **Prereq/return:** 34–41; return in 109–115, 76–82, 124. **Read More:** when a primitive clarifies or conceals. **Visual:** cd-core-l042-model-limits.

### 43. Build a familiar object from masses
**Practice (45 min):** Lightly place envelope, axis, and two/three provisional masses; compare each with measured silhouette and attachments; correct before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter household object photo. **Prereq/return:** 14–18, 34–42; return in 48–60, 47. **Read More:** construction is a proposal tested against a specific object. **Visual:** cd-core-l043-observed-construction.

### 44. Keep construction marks light and useful
**Practice (30 min):** Compare light block-in with selected contour on a container; use only helpful axes/attachments and gently lift one unnecessary guide. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–04, 34–43; return in 76–83, 92–99, 124. **Read More:** pressure can distinguish tentative structure from selected mark. **Visual:** cd-core-l044-construction-lines.

### 45. Compare construction with the observed silhouette
**Practice (45 min):** Construct a cup/tool, compare width, height, tilt, rim, and attachments with the same view, then redraw the same subject to correct its largest mismatch. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 14–18, 34–44; return in 47, 83, 99, 126. **Read More:** diagnose structural error separately from line neatness. **Visual:** cd-core-l045-rebuild.

### 46. Transfer form to a posed figure
**Practice (45 min):** Reuse gesture/proportion from 19–20; place ribcage and pelvis as broad masses on a clothed pose, compare construction with actual silhouette and balance. **Route:** Reference; fallback: self/mirror pose, no second person. **Prereq/return:** 19–20, 34–45; bridge to 72–83 and 127–130. **Read More:** masses are provisional; observed contour remains in charge. **Visual:** cd-core-l046-form-to-figure.

### 47. Extended Study — construct an unfamiliar object
**Practice (75 min):** Draw an unfamiliar joined object; plan the page, use a few masses/axes, compare envelope/openings, and rebuild the same object where the proposal fails. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 34–46; consolidates form before space; transfer at 124–126. **Read More:** unfamiliar subject is a transfer check, not a correctness test. **Visual:** cd-core-l047-form-study.

## Module 6 — Draw believable space (48–60)

**Source anchors:** N99 pp. 1–154; P16 pp. 45–55; D85 pp. 128–147. Formal perspective complements observation, relative size, vertical placement, intervals, contact, overlap, and edge/value cues.

### 48. Read depth through overlap and size
**Practice (45 min):** Draw three objects at different distances; compare overlap, relative size, and interrupted contour; correct the clearest near/far relation. **Route:** Life; fallback: foreground/background photo. **Prereq/return:** 09, 14–18, 34–47; return in 49–60, 73–83, 125. **Read More:** depth cues cooperate and may conflict. **Visual:** cd-core-l048-depth-cues.

### 49. Place forms in picture space
**Practice (45 min):** Compare vertical placement, base planes, and contacts on a shelf/floor; draw a group and correct a floating or misplaced object. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view contact-visible photo. **Prereq/return:** 15–18, 34–48; return in 52–60, 74, 83, 125–127. **Read More:** picture-plane height and ground contact without a grid. **Visual:** cd-core-l049-ground-contact.

### 50. Find your eye level and view
**Practice (45 min):** Hold one room view; compare edges that appear level, rise, or fall; mark likely eye-level relation and draw major lines. **Route:** Life; fallback: room photo from known eye height. **Prereq/return:** 08, 34–35, 48–49; return in 51–60, 125. **Read More:** eye level depends on viewpoint; horizon is not always visible. **Visual:** cd-core-l050-eye-level.

### 51. Use one-point perspective in a real room
**Practice (45 min):** Observe floor, shelf, or hallway directions; propose convergence lightly, draw broad space, compare and retain exceptions. **Route:** Reference hallway/bookshelf/room; fallback: supplied one-point view. **Prereq/return:** 48–50; return in 53, 57, 60, 125. **Read More:** one-point assumptions and real-view exceptions. **Visual:** cd-core-l051-one-point.

### 52. Turn a box in two directions
**Practice (45 min):** Draw package/book with two visible faces; compare near edge, face widths, and receding directions; use vanishing directions only if helpful and correct to view. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 34, 50–51; return in 53, 57, 125. **Read More:** vanishing points can lie off-page. **Visual:** cd-core-l052-two-point.

### 53. Place furniture and objects in a room
**Practice (45 min):** Place chair and table by relative height, overlap, and floor contact; correct spacing/support before furniture detail. **Route:** Either room or supplied interior; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 48–52; return in 57, 60, 125. **Read More:** overlap, scale, and contact work alongside perspective. **Visual:** cd-core-l053-furniture-space.

### 54. Repeated intervals recede

### 118. Put cloth on a person
**Practice (45 min):** Draw supplied seated/standing clothed figure; begin with body masses/support, identify hanging points/pull, group major folds, compare silhouette/value before creases. **Route:** reference; fallback: self/mirror in simple garment, no other person. **Prereq/return:** 73–83, 116–117; return in 127–130. No head construction or likeness is required. **Read More:** clothing changes with pose; body construction remains provisional. **Visual:** cd-core-l118-clothing-on-body.

## Module 13 — Integrate and work independently (119–130)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 10–39, 148–171; P16 pp. 26–29, 81–104; GW14 pp. 116–159; N99 pp. 1–116.

### 119. Return to your first drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Redraw Lesson 01 subject from same view; compare saved baseline for placement, silhouette, proportion, and one relation; correct the new drawing and name what changed. **Route:** Life same object/view; fallback: saved reference and sketch. **Prereq/return:** 01, 14–18, 34–47; direct spaced retrieval, not score. **Read More:** compare attempts to choose a better next question. **Visual:** cd-core-l119-baseline.

### 120. Diagnose before you polish
**Practice (45 min):** Review one old drawing with a card: frame/focal read, whole shape, proportion, angle, gap, overlap, value, edge/detail. Step back, optionally mirror/flip, choose largest error, and correct same subject/view before trying new subject. **Route:** learner drawing or supplied pair; fallback: same-view reference. **Prereq/return:** 13, 21, 33, 45, 47, 60, 72, 83; return in 126–130. **Read More:** fresh subject tests transfer, not correction of the first drawing. **Visual:** cd-core-l120-diagnosis.

### 121. Audit a photograph before drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Compare a subject in life to its photo or two photos from different views; check crop, viewpoint, exposure, focus, omissions; choose evidence and draw one large relation. **Route:** Either; fallback: paired ArtApp-owned views. **Prereq/return:** 22, 48–60, 61–72; return in 125–130. **Read More:** photograph is a projection/record, not complete neutral ground truth. **Visual:** cd-core-l121-photo-audit.

### 122. Edit an arrangement — include, omit, emphasize
**Practice (45 min):** Thumbnail three crops of a real/supplied arrangement; decide what to omit, subordinate, or emphasize; compare focal read and draw one deliberate edit. **Route:** Either; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 22–33, 91, 113; return in 123, 126–130. **Read More:** selectivity can remain faithful to chosen intent. **Visual:** cd-core-l122-edit.

### 123. Simplify shape, form, value and edge
**Practice (45 min):** Make four 3-minute thumbnails of a complex object: largest shape, simplest volume, two/three value groups, selected edges/details. Compare them with the reference, choose one, and spend the remaining time developing and correcting that version. **Route:** Either cluttered tabletop or complex scene; fallback: clear ArtApp reference. **Prereq/return:** 06, 22–47, 61–91, 109–115; return in 124–130. **Read More:** simplification changes with the drawing question. **Visual:** cd-core-l123-simplification.

### 124. Turn a familiar object and rebuild it
**Practice (45 min):** Draw one small object, rotate slightly, draw again using axes/masses provisionally; compare silhouette, openings, overlaps and correct second view rather than copying the first. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of same object. **Prereq/return:** 14–18, 34–47, 58–60; return in 126–130. **Read More:** a remembered construction must be checked anew. **Visual:** cd-core-l124-viewpoint-transfer.

### 125. Draw a room without relying on a diagram
**Practice (45 min):** Use placement, overlap, relative scale, floor contact, and intervals first; use perspective check only if it resolves an observed uncertainty; compare and correct the whole space. **Route:** Either learner room or supplied interior; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 48–60, 84–91, 121; return in 126–130. **Read More:** combine intuitive cues with formal perspective when useful. **Visual:** cd-core-l125-independent-space.

### 126. Choose the right approach in a small study
**Practice (45 min):** Select a structured subject; plan frame/big shapes, choose construction and graphite methods, draw, pause to compare/diagnose, correct same subject, decide where to stop. One main question; second optional. **Route:** Life or reference, object group/room/natural form; fallback: supplied image. **Prereq/return:** 22–33, 34–72, 84–91, 109–125; combines, little new theory. **Read More:** choose a method by subject and question. **Visual:** cd-core-l126-approach-study.

### 127. Extended Study — construct a full figure in space
**Practice (90 min):** Complete a full-body/three-quarter clothed figure in a setting, using a reference with one clearly foreshortened or overlapping limb. Plan composition/scale, apply gesture, support, proportion, landmarks, connected volumes, and hands/feet; compare and correct the same pose. **Route:** supplied matching figure/environment; fallback: compatible separate refs or self/mirror. **Prereq/return:** 19–20, 46, 48–60, 72–83, 100–108, 121–126; late form-to-anatomy transfer. **Read More:** sequence figure and setting without turning either into a diagram. **Visual:** cd-core-l127-figure-space.

### 128. Transfer likeness into clothing
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a half- or three-quarter crop of one specific clothed person; construct head and torso lightly, observe individual features and major fold directions, group values, then correct the largest likeness or structure error on the same view. Show hands/feet only if they fall within the chosen crop. **Route:** supplied person; fallback: self/mirror portrait and simple garment. **Prereq/return:** 73–83, 92–99, 116–118, 120–127; hand/foot studies 100–108 are useful only when the chosen crop includes them. **Read More:** choose which error to correct first. **Visual:** cd-core-l128-likeness-drapery.

### 129. Plan an independent drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Choose a subject and one main question; decide life/reference, crop, composition, construction, graphite marks, and omissions. Thumbnail and begin, revising the plan if observation changes the question. **Route:** learner choice; fallback: supplied reference. **Prereq/return:** 91, 119–128; learner now chooses checks. **Read More:** planning is flexible, not a mechanical checklist. **Visual:** cd-core-l129-plan.

### 130. Extended Study — Independent Core Drawing — draw, diagnose, decide
**Practice (90 min):** Complete learner-chosen life/reference drawing. Plan page, select methods, draw, step back, compare shape/space/value/focal read, correct one priority on same subject, and stop deliberately. One main question is enough. **Route:** learner choice; fallback: same-view ArtApp reference. **Prereq/return:** spiral of 01–129, not a mastery gate. **Read More:** record useful choices and one next question; completion is a beginning, not mastery. **Visual:** cd-core-l130-independent.
