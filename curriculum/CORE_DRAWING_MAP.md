# ArtApp Core Drawing — 150-lesson curriculum architecture

**Canonical final architecture; planning only; architecture approved in broad structure and content-complete after a targeted Landscape & Nature expansion.** This supersedes the previous 130-lesson architecture and earlier 93-lesson plan. No further Core content expansion is planned after this pass. The sequence was rebuilt conceptually from a blank Lesson 01. Its count emerged from practice and transfer needs; it is not a quota. This map is not lesson copy, lesson JSON, app implementation, or approval to generate teaching images.

**Pilot status:** The published pilot JSON and assets for legacy Lessons 01–09 are prototypes/reference material only. Their IDs, order, subjects, exercises, visuals, and production investment do not constrain this course. Keep them unchanged as production references while the approved architecture moves into production; later reuse or replace individual ideas only on their teaching merit. Sequence numbers below do not reserve pilot content IDs.

## Design and use

- Start with a recognizable subject and drawing in Lesson 01. Teach only HB, comfortable hold, light first marks, gentle correction, and avoiding grooves in the paper.
- Use draw → observe → compare → diagnose → correct → redraw. Construction proposes; the observed subject/reference decides whether it fits.
- Graphite is a continuous strand: introduce a mark, immediately apply it, revisit, deepen, combine, then let the learner choose. Two-value grouping, side tone, even tone, basic directional hatching, layering, simple gradation, and preserving paper white are taught in Lessons 31–34, before full value study.
- Composition is explicitly taught in 23–35 and revisited in later arrangements, portraits, rooms, landscapes, and extended studies; it is not forced into every technical exercise.
- Form progresses from observed solids to connected and modified volumes, then transfers to space, people, heads, hands, feet, natural forms, trees, rocks, terrain, landscape, cloth, and independent studies.
- Outdoor drawing receives early transfer practice and a focused 14-lesson foundation after its prerequisites. Core teaches manageable trees, terrain, rocks, outdoor depth, value, sky, ordinary water, built/natural integration, field sketching and a guided landscape; advanced landscape rendering remains specialist study.
- Life lessons have a low-friction setup and a named fixed-view reference fallback. Lesson 01 introduces photo use before the first fallback: choose a clear, uncropped view, note crop/viewpoint/light, and keep that view fixed while drawing. Lesson 141 develops a fuller photo audit. Human lessons use supplied references, self/mirror routes, or an optional willing model; no lesson requires another person. Every outdoor lesson has an ArtApp-owned fixed-reference fallback; going outside is encouraged but never mandatory. ArtApp-owned supplied references may use Welsh landscapes or environments where suitable, without making that scenery a learner requirement.
- Focused Study = 30 minutes; Standard = 45; Extended = 75–90. Setup, drawing, comparison, correction, and review fit inside the time. Extended studies consolidate material already taught.
- HB completes every lesson. Extra grades and tools are optional. Core is graphite only; charcoal, color, detailed anatomy, specialist botanical work, and advanced landscape rendering remain outside this course.
- Read More is optional original ArtApp teaching. Sources & Further Reading is separate and records real influence. Books, scans, extracts, analyses, and notes remain private under sources/.
- Each lesson entry gives practice, route/fallback, prerequisites and returns, Read More intent, and a visual ID. Module source anchors apply to the listed lessons. Detailed image prompts are in CORE_DRAWING_VISUAL_MANIFEST.md.

## Module 1 — Start looking and marking (01–05)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 10–69; WG18 pp. 15–37; P16 pp. 8–9, 21–23, 31–41; A11 Ch. 3.

### 01. Draw one real thing — look, mark, compare, change
**Practice (45 min):** Learner chooses a simple object, draws immediately with HB, compares one visible relation, and corrects it. Teach comfortable writing hold, light first marks, gentle erasing, and no paper grooves; label and keep this baseline for 139. If using a photo, check that the whole object/contact is clear, note crop, viewpoint and light, then keep that view fixed. Choose a reusable object/view or keep a quick setup photo so the baseline can be repeated. **Route:** Life, spoon/fruit/cup/package; fallback: fixed-view simple ArtApp reference. **Prereq/return:** none; revisit same view at 139, independent cycle at 150. **Read More:** first drawing as useful evidence, not a talent test. **Visual:** cd-core-l001-observation-cycle.

### 02. Keep the first marks light — pressure and paper
**Practice (30 min):** Calibrate light/medium/firm pressure on scrap, then make and correct a light block-in on a box or book; check the reverse for dents and place a spare sheet under the hand. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view photo, with pressure practice on the learner’s paper. **Prereq/return:** 01; return in 31–34, 47–49, 66–76, 89. **Read More:** pressure versus confidence and why reversible marks help. **Visual:** cd-core-l002-pressure-paper.

### 03. Choose point or side — angle, rotation, sharpening
**Practice (45 min):** Compare pointed contour and broad side marks at two pencil angles; rotate a rounded point and sharpen only when detail needs it; apply choices to fruit or a smooth object. HB only. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired close-up reference plus mark test. **Prereq/return:** 02; return in 32–34, 66–70, 89–96. **Read More:** pencil position changes contact; grip is functional, not fixed. **Visual:** cd-core-l003-point-side.

### 04. Search then select — line and correction
**Practice (45 min):** Make faint searching lines to locate a spoon/tool, compare, select a clearer contour, and correct the same drawing. Teach ordinary eraser and putty/kneaded eraser if available; single-eraser route uses a gentle scrap test. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view tool photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–03; return in 05, 47, 89, 97, 150. **Read More:** searching is evidence gathering; compare erasing, lifting, and leaving a guide. **Visual:** cd-core-l004-search-select.

### 05. Follow an edge — contour direction and turns
**Practice (45 min):** Draw several changing boundary directions on a shoe, shell, or tool; compare and correct two turns on the same subject before transferring one turn to a second object. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched side-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 01–04; return in 06–14, 36–50, 89–91. **Read More:** contour is selected; it may vanish at overlap or into value. **Visual:** cd-core-l005-contour-turns.

## Module 2 — Shape, gaps and overlap (06–14)

**Source anchors:** WG18 pp. 24–71; E12 PDF pp. 121–211; P16 pp. 26–41, 57–63; D85 pp. 128–147.

### 06. See the outside shape first
**Practice (30 min):** Draw a parcel, toy, or fruit as one envelope, add two large divisions, compare height/width/tilt, and correct the outside before parts. **Route:** Life; fallback: neutral single-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 05; return in 11–14, 36–50, 143. **Read More:** what silhouette can and cannot tell you. **Visual:** cd-core-l006-silhouette.

### 07. Read a tree as a silhouette and a set of gaps
**Practice (30 min):** Make two small drawings from one observed or fixed-reference tree: first reduce the tree or tree group to its outer shape, then use two or three branch gaps to check the silhouette. Compare a vertical and a wide crop; omit leaf marks and individual twigs. **Route:** Life where practical; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view tree or tree-group reference. **Prereq/return:** 06; return in 12, 128–130, 138–150. **Read More:** tree silhouettes are observed shapes, and negative spaces can expose a remembered tree symbol. This first branch-gap check previews, but does not replace, the dedicated negative-space lesson in 08. **Visual:** cd-core-l007-tree-silhouette.

### 08. Draw the spaces around it
**Practice (45 min):** Use gaps around a chair or plant to check subject shape; compare gap angles/widths and correct the surrounding contour, without shading the gaps. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–06; return in 12, 16, 26, 140, 143. **Read More:** positive and negative shapes share an edge. **Visual:** cd-core-l008-negative-shapes.

### 09. Compare slants and alignments
**Practice (45 min):** Find three directions in scissors, a tool, or chair; compare tilt and one plumb alignment with a pencil; redraw the largest error. **Route:** Life; fallback: top-down arrangement photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–08; return in 17–19, 38–48, 51–64, 81–87. **Read More:** direction and alignment depend on viewpoint. **Visual:** cd-core-l009-angle-alignment.

### 10. Show which form overlaps
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a jar behind a carton; locate interrupted contours, add only useful faint through-lines, then correct the overlap and one tangency. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 05–09; return in 28, 44–45, 51–61, 108–113. **Read More:** T-junctions are evidence of occlusion, not a universal rule. **Visual:** cd-core-l010-overlap.

### 11. Divide a large shape into a few parts
**Practice (30 min):** Draw the envelope of a kettle, bottle, or packaged object, compare two or three large internal shapes, and revise one boundary without small detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: clear reference with visible divisions. **Prereq/return:** 06–10; return in 46–50, 98–105, 115–121. **Read More:** grouping; distinguish an edge from a value change. **Visual:** cd-core-l011-large-shapes.

### 12. Repeat and compare the gaps
**Practice (45 min):** Draw three books, cups, or shelf divisions; compare spaces between them as shapes and correct one interval on the same page. **Route:** Life; fallback: straight-on photo. **Prereq/return:** 08–11; return in 18–19, 26, 56–58, 143. **Read More:** repeated intervals help; equal real gaps may look unequal. **Visual:** cd-core-l012-repeated-gaps.

### 13. Read a contour through an interruption
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a handle behind a rim or a stem through leaves in visible sections; compare breaks with overlap and correct two interruptions before interior marks. **Route:** Life; fallback: high-contrast same-view reference. **Prereq/return:** 05, 08, 10–12; return in 44–45, 91, 119, 146. **Read More:** distinguish observed edge from imagined continuation. **Visual:** cd-core-l013-interrupted-contour.

### 14. Extended Study — two objects, one clear drawing
**Practice (75 min):** Set up two overlapping objects; plan placement, draw silhouette/gaps, pause to compare width, tilt, spacing, and overlap, then correct the largest issue. Keep the drawing. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied two-object photo. **Prereq/return:** 06–13; consolidates shape; baseline recheck at 139. **Read More:** select one useful correction over polishing every line. **Visual:** cd-core-l014-shape-study.

## Module 3 — Measure and begin figure observation (15–22)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 40–69; E12 PDF pp. 180–211; P16 pp. 26–29, 42–63; H09 pp. 3–28.

### 15. Choose a measuring unit
**Practice (45 min):** At a steady arm’s-length viewpoint, sight the object’s height as a temporary unit with a pencil/thumb mark; compare its width and one feature against that unit, transfer the ratio to a light block-in, and return to the same position between checks. **Route:** Life, bottle/book/appliance; fallback: same-view photo (compare visible ratios, not screen dimensions). **Prereq/return:** 06–14; return in 16–19, 21, 47–50, 80. **Read More:** comparative measurement is relational, not numeric perfection. **Visual:** cd-core-l015-unit-measure.

### 16. Check the whole — height, width and placement
**Practice (30 min):** Place height and width in a page frame, compare margins and widest point, correct envelope, then add two large shapes. **Route:** Life, box and round object; fallback: single-view image. **Prereq/return:** 15; return in 23–35, 139–150. **Read More:** page placement affects the read of an otherwise accurate object. **Visual:** cd-core-l016-whole-placement.

### 17. Check a long angle and alignment
**Practice (45 min):** Compare two major axes on an irregular object using a pencil sighting guide; check a plumb relation and redraw one direction. **Route:** Life, scissors/tool; fallback: matching photo. **Prereq/return:** 09, 15–16; return in 38–49, 53–58, 81–87. **Read More:** angle and length checks answer different questions. **Visual:** cd-core-l017-sighted-angles.

### 18. Compare repeated intervals
**Practice (45 min):** Measure three spaces in a row of jars, books, or shelf divisions; compare to the first interval and redraw the group without making every picture gap equal. **Route:** Life; fallback: straight-on reference. **Prereq/return:** 12, 15–17; return in 56–58, 145. **Read More:** interval is a shape and sometimes a depth clue. **Visual:** cd-core-l018-intervals.

### 19. Measure an irregular object
**Practice (45 min):** Compare envelope, axis, widest point, and two landmarks on a shoe, vegetable, or tool; use a light block-in and correct the whole before contour. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo with one clear axis. **Prereq/return:** 15–18; return in 115–121, 144. **Read More:** measurement supports irregular observation without regularizing it. **Visual:** cd-core-l019-irregular-measure.

### 20. Catch a pose in a few lines
**Practice (30 min):** Draw supplied short poses using an action line and two direction changes; compare pose direction and revise one line, without anatomy construction. **Route:** Reference, clothed adult poses; fallback: self/mirror pose or supplied sequence, never another person. **Prereq/return:** 09, 15–17; return in 21, 49, 76–88, 147, 150. **Read More:** gesture records action, not a stick-figure template. **Visual:** cd-core-l020-gesture.

### 21. Compare the pose — proportion and balance
**Practice (45 min):** Make two small full-figure block-ins from standing/seated poses; compare height/width, torso/leg relation, tilt, and ground contact; correct one proportion in each. No universal head-count template. **Route:** Reference; fallback: mirror/self or supplied poses. **Prereq/return:** 15–20; return in 49, 79–88, 147. **Read More:** proportion varies with pose and person. **Visual:** cd-core-l021-pose-proportion.

### 22. Study — measure a small arrangement
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a three-object still life; choose one unit, place the group, compare gaps/intervals, and correct the largest error before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied fixed-view arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 15–19; consolidates observation/measurement before composition. **Read More:** keep one comparison question active through a drawing. **Visual:** cd-core-l022-measured-study.

## Module 4 — Compose, simplify and use early tone (23–35)

**Source anchors:** GW14 pp. 14–70, 116–159; D85 pp. 148–197; P16 pp. 65–79, 81–95.

### 23. Frame the view — crop and scale
**Practice (30 min):** Make three crops of one arrangement, compare wide/close/off-centre readings, choose one, and draw a larger study preserving its boundaries. **Route:** Either life setup or photo; fallback: three matched crops. **Prereq/return:** 16, 22; return in 27–31, 35, 97, 141–143, 150. **Read More:** framing changes meaning without changing objects. **Visual:** cd-core-l023-crop-scale.

### 24. Crop a simple outdoor view
**Practice (30 min):** Make three thumbnail crops of an outdoor view or ArtApp-owned reference. Compare sky/land division, dominant silhouette, and whether a path, wall, or tree group helps the eye enter; choose one crop and draw only its largest shapes. **Route:** Outdoor life drawing is encouraged; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view landscape image. **Prereq/return:** 23; return in 125, 142–150. **Read More:** a crop is a choice about what the drawing will explain, not a promise to include every visible feature. **Visual:** cd-core-l024-outdoor-crop.

### 25. Place the subject on the page
**Practice (45 min):** Compare two placements of a familiar group; check margins, visual weight, and empty space; redraw the chosen placement at sketchbook size. **Route:** Either still life/photo; fallback: same-view image. **Prereq/return:** 16, 23; return in 35, 105, 142–150. **Read More:** centring is an option, not a rule. **Visual:** cd-core-l025-page-placement.

### 26. Balance positive and negative shapes
**Practice (45 min):** Compare gaps around three objects; adjust their arrangement if useful and compare revised gaps and silhouettes. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo with clear spaces. **Prereq/return:** 08, 23–25; return in 51–61, 108–112, 140. **Read More:** balance is a relationship, not a formula. **Visual:** cd-core-l026-space-balance.

### 27. Give the eye a place to begin
**Practice (45 min):** Make three thumbnails changing relative size, placement, or overlap; compare focal read and subordination. Required choices use placement/scale; value waits until Lesson 31. **Route:** Either, fruit and household object/photo; fallback: same arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 23–26; return in 34–35, 97, 104–105, 142. **Read More:** visual weight and hierarchy without a fixed recipe. **Visual:** cd-core-l027-focal-hierarchy.

### 28. Arrange depth with overlap and scale
**Practice (45 min):** Make two arrangements changing overlap; draw the clearer one and correct silhouette/ground contact before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of the objects. **Prereq/return:** 10, 23–27; return in 51–61, 79–88, 108–113. **Read More:** overlap and scale as observed depth and composition. **Visual:** cd-core-l028-depth-arrangement.

### 29. Find and fix a tangency
**Practice (45 min):** Diagnose near-touching contours as clear, ambiguous, or useful; change a life setup or test one thumbnail edit on a fixed image. **Route:** Either objects or photo; fallback: same-view image. **Prereq/return:** 10, 23–28; return in 97, 101, 142. **Read More:** tangency is a clarity question, not a ban on close edges. **Visual:** cd-core-l029-tangency.

### 30. Follow a direction through the frame
**Practice (45 min):** Find a path/rhythm in tools or a natural cluster; thumbnail how the eye travels, then draw major shapes without outlining every item. **Route:** Life; fallback: reference with visible rhythm. **Prereq/return:** 23–29; return in 35, 119–121, 142, 148. **Read More:** repeated angles, curves, and spacing can guide attention. **Visual:** cd-core-l030-rhythm.

### 31. Two values on a real object
**Practice (45 min):** Use one steady window or lamp from a single side; test a broad side tone and light parallel strokes, group a pear, cup, or carton into light and shadow, reserve paper white, compare, and correct the boundary. **Route:** Life under fixed light; fallback: fixed-light photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–03, 23–30; first tonal use, deepened in 32–34 and 65–76. **Read More:** simple reversible marks and two-value grouping. **Visual:** cd-core-l031-two-value.

### 32. Make an even tone and directional hatch
**Practice (45 min):** Build a short even band with light layers, then use one directional hatch on a visible plane/surface of a familiar object; compare pressure with repeated layers and avoid dents. **Route:** Life carton/book/fruit; fallback: simple two-value image. **Prereq/return:** 02–03, 31; return in 67–70, 89–96. **Read More:** stroke spacing and direction are choices. **Visual:** cd-core-l032-even-tone-hatch.

### 33. Try a simple gradation and preserve the light
**Practice (45 min):** Build a light-to-dark transition with pressure/layers, apply it to the observed turn of an egg or fruit, leave paper for the brightest light, and compare rather than fill every surface. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-light close-up. **Prereq/return:** 31–32; return in 38, 66–75, 120–124. **Read More:** distinguish observed gradation from a mechanically smooth strip. **Visual:** cd-core-l033-gradation.

### 34. Plan a focal value thumbnail
**Practice (45 min):** Reduce an arrangement to two/three value masses; thumbnail two dark placements, compare hierarchy, then apply the better plan to a short recognizable drawing. **Route:** Either life still life or photo; fallback: same-view crop. **Prereq/return:** 27, 31–33; deepen in 65–76, 103–105, 142. **Read More:** value grouping and focal emphasis. **Visual:** cd-core-l034-value-thumbnail.

### 35. Extended Study — compose a small still life
**Practice (75 min):** Arrange objects, compare three crops, choose a hierarchy, draw big shapes and a two/three-value plan, then pause for page/gap/focal review and one correction. **Route:** Life under simple light; fallback: supplied arrangement. **Prereq/return:** 23–34; consolidates composition and early graphite before form. **Read More:** omit a visible detail that weakens the read. **Visual:** cd-core-l035-composition-study.

## Module 5 — Construct observed form (36–50)

**Source anchors:** P16 pp. 57–63; D85 pp. 128–147; N99 pp. 21–66, 117–154; A11 Ch. 5.

### 36. A solid has planes
**Practice (45 min):** Propose front/side/top planes on a package; compare widths and visible edges; keep only useful guides. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 09, 15–17; return in 38–50, 54–64, 145. **Read More:** boxes model space but the observed view may resist ideal geometry. **Visual:** cd-core-l036-box-planes.

### 37. Build a rock from mass and planes
**Practice (45 min):** Draw one palm-sized rock or supplied boulder reference as a large uneven mass, then locate two or three plane changes and its ground contact. Compare the drawing with the observed silhouette; delay cracks and surface marks until the mass reads. **Route:** Life rock or irregular household object; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view rock reference. **Prereq/return:** 36; return in 131, 138, 143–150. **Read More:** borrow plane thinking without forcing a rock into a box or identifying its geology. **Visual:** cd-core-l037-rock-mass.

### 38. Find the axis through a rounded form
**Practice (30 min):** Mark long axis and cross-section of an egg or bottle; compare axis with silhouette and select contour lightly. **Route:** Life; fallback: close three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 15–17, 36; return in 39–41, 115–121, 81–87. **Read More:** axes are orientation checks, not required final marks. **Visual:** cd-core-l038-axis.

### 39. Read an ellipse — axis and opening
**Practice (45 min):** Compare a bowl rim from two eye heights; draw its axis and apparent opening, then compare to the actual rim rather than a template. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of same dish. **Prereq/return:** 36–38; return in 40–41, 62–63, 109–111. **Read More:** ellipse degree follows viewing orientation. **Visual:** cd-core-l039-ellipse.

### 40. Build a cylinder from its end planes
**Practice (45 min):** Draw axis, openings, and sides of a tube or bottle; compare taper, rim width, and axis; correct before darkening. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired height views. **Prereq/return:** 36–39; return in 41–50, 62–63, 84–87. **Read More:** aligned end planes help, but objects are not always true cylinders. **Visual:** cd-core-l040-cylinder.

### 41. Turn an egg and sphere
**Practice (45 min):** Use a few cross-contours to show turn; compare with silhouette and light, revise one line that makes the form read flat. **Route:** Life under ordinary light; fallback: two fixed views. **Prereq/return:** 38–40; return in 115–121, 98–103, 70–73. **Read More:** cross-contour is checked imagination, not decoration. **Visual:** cd-core-l041-round-volume.

### 42. Make a tapered form
**Practice (45 min):** Compare axis and changing width of funnel, carrot, or tapered container; build broad-to-narrow volume and correct silhouette/opening. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo along its axis. **Prereq/return:** 36–41; return in 43–46, 115–118. **Read More:** taper is a volume change, not an outline trick. **Visual:** cd-core-l042-taper.

### 43. Join and intersect simple volumes
**Practice (45 min):** Find two or three masses in a watering can, lamp, or stapler; mark axes/attachments, observe overlap, and correct one join. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 36–42; return in 44–50, 81–87, 106–114, 123–124. **Read More:** attachment, overlap, and merging are different relationships. **Visual:** cd-core-l043-joined-forms.

### 44. Draw through, then select what is visible
**Practice (45 min):** Use faint through-lines on a chair or table; compare hidden guides with actual overlap, strengthen visible edges and leave hidden structure pale/erased. **Route:** Either; fallback: three-quarter chair photo. **Prereq/return:** 10, 36, 40, 43; return in 51–61, 83–88, 89–91. **Read More:** hidden construction checks, not mandatory visible lines. **Visual:** cd-core-l044-draw-through.

### 45. Use a simple form without forcing it
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a cup, kettle, or appliance partly resembling a box/cylinder; mark where the model stops helping and adjust it to observed asymmetry. **Route:** Life; fallback: matched photo. **Prereq/return:** 36–44; return in 115–121, 81–87, 144. **Read More:** when a primitive clarifies or conceals. **Visual:** cd-core-l045-model-limits.

### 46. Build a familiar object from masses
**Practice (45 min):** Lightly place envelope, axis, and two/three provisional masses; compare each with measured silhouette and attachments; correct before detail. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter household object photo. **Prereq/return:** 15–19, 36–45; return in 51–64, 50. **Read More:** construction is a proposal tested against a specific object. **Visual:** cd-core-l046-observed-construction.

### 47. Keep construction marks light and useful
**Practice (30 min):** Compare light block-in with selected contour on a container; use only helpful axes/attachments and gently lift one unnecessary guide. **Route:** Life; fallback: fixed-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 02–04, 36–46; return in 81–88, 98–105, 144. **Read More:** pressure can distinguish tentative structure from selected mark. **Visual:** cd-core-l047-construction-lines.

### 48. Compare construction with the observed silhouette
**Practice (45 min):** Construct a cup/tool, compare width, height, tilt, rim, and attachments with the same view, then redraw the same subject to correct its largest mismatch. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 15–19, 36–47; return in 50, 88, 105, 146. **Read More:** diagnose structural error separately from line neatness. **Visual:** cd-core-l048-rebuild.

### 49. Transfer form to a posed figure
**Practice (45 min):** Reuse gesture/proportion from 20–21; place ribcage and pelvis as broad masses on a clothed pose, compare construction with actual silhouette and balance. **Route:** Reference; fallback: self/mirror pose, no second person. **Prereq/return:** 20–21, 36–48; bridge to 78–88 and 147–150. **Read More:** masses are provisional; observed contour remains in charge. **Visual:** cd-core-l049-form-to-figure.

### 50. Extended Study — construct an unfamiliar object
**Practice (75 min):** Draw an unfamiliar joined object; plan the page, use a few masses/axes, compare envelope/openings, and rebuild the same object where the proposal fails. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 36–49; consolidates form before space; transfer at 144–146. **Read More:** unfamiliar subject is a transfer check, not a correctness test. **Visual:** cd-core-l050-form-study.

## Module 6 — Draw believable space (51–64)

**Source anchors:** N99 pp. 1–154; P16 pp. 45–55; D85 pp. 128–147. Formal perspective complements observation, relative size, vertical placement, intervals, contact, overlap, and edge/value cues.

### 51. Read depth through overlap and size
**Practice (45 min):** Draw three objects at different distances; compare overlap, relative size, and interrupted contour; correct the clearest near/far relation. **Route:** Life; fallback: foreground/background photo. **Prereq/return:** 10, 15–19, 36–50; return in 52–64, 78–88, 145. **Read More:** depth cues cooperate and may conflict. **Visual:** cd-core-l051-depth-cues.

### 52. Place forms in picture space
**Practice (45 min):** Compare vertical placement, base planes, and contacts on a shelf/floor; draw a group and correct a floating or misplaced object. **Route:** Life; fallback: same-view contact-visible photo. **Prereq/return:** 16–19, 36–51; return in 55–64, 79, 88, 145–147. **Read More:** picture-plane height and ground contact without a grid. **Visual:** cd-core-l052-ground-contact.

### 53. Find your eye level and view

### 116. Describe swelling, taper and irregular contour
**Practice (45 min):** Compare where a natural form swells, narrows, bends, or changes surface; draw mass and a few contour changes, correct its distinctive asymmetry. **Route:** Life pepper/root vegetable/shell/branch; fallback: close-up. **Prereq/return:** 38–45, 115; return in 117–121, 143–146. **Read More:** structure as change along an axis. **Visual:** cd-core-l116-swell-taper.

### 117. Follow branching forms from trunk to major branches
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a small tree or branch study from its trunk axis and taper through two or three major forks. Compare branch direction, hierarchy, attachment, overlap, and asymmetry with observation; correct one junction without building a symmetrical formula or tracing every twig. **Route:** Life; fallback: ArtApp-owned close view with clear trunk/branch junctions. **Prereq/return:** 09–13, 38–45, 115–116; return in 118–121, 128, 142. **Read More:** branching is connected direction under observation, not a repeated pattern. **Visual:** cd-core-l117-branching.

### 118. Cross-contour on an irregular surface
**Practice (45 min):** Add a few cross-contours to shell/root/natural form; compare to visible bulges/planes and remove a line that implies the wrong volume. **Route:** Life; fallback: three-quarter photo. **Prereq/return:** 38–41, 115–117; relate to form-following marks in 92, then return in 119–121 and 131. **Read More:** cross-contour tests volume and may be omitted. **Visual:** cd-core-l118-organic-cross-contour.

### 119. Group an organic cluster
**Practice (45 min):** Draw leaves, stones, or food by silhouette, axes, and negative gaps; group details, correct the whole before texture. Flowers optional. **Route:** Life; fallback: small cluster photo. **Prereq/return:** 08, 12, 30, 115–118; return in 120–121, 142–143. **Read More:** grouping without turning plants into a specialist subject. **Visual:** cd-core-l119-organic-group.

### 120. Let surface marks follow natural form
**Practice (45 min):** Select lines/value changes that follow natural surface; compare direction and remove texture that competes with silhouette. **Route:** Life fruit/shell/branch/stone; fallback: close photo. **Prereq/return:** 65–75, 92–96, 115–119; return in 121, 124, 146. **Read More:** selective surface description can reinforce or flatten volume. **Visual:** cd-core-l120-natural-surface.

### 121. Extended Study — build an irregular subject
**Practice (75 min):** Choose natural object/group; use axis/mass, preserve asymmetry, compare silhouette/gaps, simplify, and correct structure before selective tone/texture. **Route:** Life; fallback: supplied reference. **Prereq/return:** 115–120; tests geometric-to-organic transfer; revisit in 143–146. **Read More:** notice when construction over-regularizes. **Visual:** cd-core-l121-organic-study.

### 122. Explain folds through gravity and support
**Practice (45 min):** Hang towel/light cloth over chair or object; mark support/hanging points and compare major fold directions to gravity/tension; correct cause/effect, not wrinkles. **Route:** Life; fallback: photo with support points. **Prereq/return:** 36–45, 78–88, 115–121; return in 123–124, 148. **Read More:** gravity, support, tension, compression cause folds. **Visual:** cd-core-l122-fold-causes.

### 123. Draw major folds over a simple form
**Practice (45 min):** Drape cloth over box/ball/chair; identify pull, compression, support, and major fold families; compare value groups before small creases. **Route:** Life; fallback: image tying folds to support. **Prereq/return:** 40–45, 65–75, 90–95, 122; return in 124, 148. **Read More:** folds wrap volume; every wrinkle is not equally useful. **Visual:** cd-core-l123-folds-over-form.

### 124. Put cloth on a person
**Practice (45 min):** Draw supplied seated/standing clothed figure; begin with body masses/support, identify hanging points/pull, group major folds, compare silhouette/value before creases. **Route:** reference; fallback: self/mirror in simple garment, no other person. **Prereq/return:** 78–88, 122–123; return in 147–150. No head construction or likeness is required. **Read More:** clothing changes with pose; body construction remains provisional. **Visual:** cd-core-l124-clothing-on-body.

## Module 13 — Landscape & Nature foundations (125–138)

**Purpose:** Combine observation, composition, construction, space, value, edge, and organic-form skills to approach manageable outdoor scenes. Landscape is a selected drawing problem, not an inventory. Every life route has an ArtApp-owned fixed-reference fallback. The module includes tree structure before foliage grouping, calm water before moving water, and a guided sustained drawing before the later independent course ending.

### 125. Edit a landscape into a few connected shapes
**Practice (45 min):** Choose one manageable outdoor view or fixed reference. Make three quick crops, mark the sky/land division if visible, identify the largest connected shapes and directional lines, then combine or omit details that do not help the chosen focal read. Ask what the largest shape is, which masses can join, what identifies this place, where contrast and a useful edge sit, and what can disappear. Develop one thumbnail as a clear shape drawing and compare it with the view; an ordinary wall, path, bridge, or building may join natural forms if present. **Route:** Outdoor drawing encouraged; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view landscape reference. **Prereq/return:** 23–35, 51–64, 65–77, 89–97, 115–121; return in 142–150. **Read More:** landscape drawing is a designed selection; scene detail that does not serve the chosen read is optional. **Visual:** cd-core-l125-landscape-edit.

### 126. Establish foreground, middle ground and distance
**Practice (45 min):** Make a small scene drawing that groups near, middle, and far shapes where the view supports them. Use overlap, relative scale, vertical placement, receding intervals, and convergence only when observed; compare the same scene with one cue removed to see what still carries depth. Use paths or field boundaries where present, but do not force every scene into three bands. **Route:** Life or supplied landscape; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view reference with visible depth cues. **Prereq/return:** 51–64, 125; return in 127, 133, 138, 145–150. **Read More:** formal perspective is one optional cue among overlap, scale, placement, and interval. **Visual:** cd-core-l126-landscape-depth.

### 127. Read terrain and ground planes
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a simple slope, hill, bank, or receding path as a few connected ground forms. Include one ordinary built boundary—such as a wall, fence, road, or path—alongside surrounding ground or vegetation; compare how both follow the slope and correct one spatial relation. Use contour and a few cross-contours to show plane changes. Add a small value grouping only after the land shape reads. **Route:** Life or reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view slope or bank showing a built boundary with natural forms. **Prereq/return:** 36–50, 51–64, 126; return in 131–133, 138, 145–150. **Read More:** terrain is a surface that turns and recedes, not a flat strip under objects; avoid abstract perspective diagrams. **Visual:** cd-core-l127-terrain.

### 128. Construct a tree from trunk to canopy
**Practice (45 min):** Make two short tree block-ins from observation: mark each trunk gesture/axis and taper, place a few major branch directions and attachments, then add only the largest canopy masses. Compare branching hierarchy, overlap, asymmetry, silhouette, and two useful negative gaps; correct the trunk-to-canopy relationship before surface marks. **Route:** Life; fallback: ArtApp-owned tree reference with visible trunk and branch structure. **Prereq/return:** 36–50, 115–124; return in 129–130, 138, 145–150. **Read More:** construction proposes the tree's structure; the observed individual tree decides its shape. **Visual:** cd-core-l128-tree-structure.

### 129. Group foliage as canopy masses
**Practice (45 min):** Turn one observed canopy into a few volume groups with light/shadow families, outer silhouette, and selected internal gaps. Reveal a branch only where it helps; vary hard, soft, and lost/found edges, then add a few marks that suggest leaf clusters and concentrate detail in one useful area. Compare a mass-first version with a leaf-by-leaf version and correct the mass-first study. **Route:** Life tree or supplied reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view canopy study. **Prereq/return:** 65–77, 89–97, 128; return in 130, 132–134, 138, 145–150. **Read More:** describe foliage through volume, spacing, light, and selective texture rather than thousands of leaves. **Visual:** cd-core-l129-foliage-masses.

### 130. Organize a tree group or woodland edge
**Practice (45 min):** Make two thumbnails of several trees: combine them as one connected mass in one version, then separate overlapping near/far groups in the other. Compare internal gaps, repeated rhythms, silhouette, and the directional shape of the group; develop the clearer arrangement and leave some trees indistinct. **Route:** Life or reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned treeline or woodland-edge reference. **Prereq/return:** 23–35, 126, 128–129; return in 132–134, 138, 142–150. **Read More:** individual trees can serve one larger compositional shape. **Visual:** cd-core-l130-tree-groups.

### 131. Construct rocks and irregular outdoor masses
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a boulder, rock group, or rocky bank as a large mass with observed orientation, overlap, contact, and two or three major planes. Group light and shadow, then select only a few cracks or texture marks that support the planes and scale; compare and correct the whole before detail. **Route:** Life or reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view rock group. **Prereq/return:** 37, 65–77, 115–124, 127; return in 132, 138, 145–150. **Read More:** use construction to describe irregular outdoor form without geological classification or invented facets. **Visual:** cd-core-l131-rocks.

### 132. Organize landscape values before detail
**Practice (45 min):** Make a three- or four-value thumbnail of a simple landscape, grouping only the large sky, land, tree, building, foreground-shadow, or water masses that occur in the view. Compare a strong tree-against-sky or light-building-against-vegetation relationship, subordinate less important shapes, then draw one short value study and correct the broadest mismatch before texture. **Route:** Life or fixed reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned landscape image with clear large value shapes. **Prereq/return:** 31–34, 65–77, 125–131; return in 133–138, 142–150. **Read More:** large value relationships lead, followed by secondary form and selective detail; foreground is not automatically the darkest. **Visual:** cd-core-l132-landscape-value.

### 133. Show atmospheric distance through observation
**Practice (45 min):** Compare a near, middle, and distant mass where the view contains them. Draw the same small scene twice: first with equal detail and contrast, then with only the visible changes to value range, edge clarity, detail, and shape complexity. Compare both with the reference and revise any mechanically lightened, blurred, or otherwise altered distance treatment. **Route:** Life or fixed reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned landscape reference with layered distance. **Prereq/return:** 51–64, 65–77, 89–97, 126, 132; return in 138, 142–150. **Read More:** atmospheric cues vary with weather, humidity, lighting, distance, and photo exposure; observation decides. **Visual:** cd-core-l133-atmospheric-distance.

### 134. Draw sky and clouds as landscape masses
**Practice (45 min):** In a small landscape crop, compare how much of the frame belongs to sky and land. Preserve paper white where useful, group a broad sky value if observed, and treat clouds as a few massed light/shadow shapes with changing soft and defined edges. Add one restrained cloud study without outlining every cloud or giving it a symbol. **Route:** Observe a stable sky briefly or use a reference; fallback: ArtApp-owned sky/land image with fixed light. **Prereq/return:** 23–35, 65–77, 89–97, 125, 132–133; return in 138, 142–150. **Read More:** note the sky's overall arrangement promptly; clouds change, and no meteorological naming is needed. **Visual:** cd-core-l134-sky-clouds.

### 135. Observe still and gently moving water
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a pond, lake edge, sheltered river, or canal as a surface plane. First map the broad reflected shapes and value masses beneath their sources; then compare how the water breaks, softens, or distorts those shapes and where the surface interrupts them. Correct the large reflected relationship before adding a few selective ripple marks; do not make a reversed photocopy or repeat symbolic horizontal lines. **Route:** Life observation where practical; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view water reference with visible source and reflection. **Prereq/return:** 51–64, 65–77, 89–97, 126, 132; return in 136; optionally revisit in 138 or a learner-chosen landscape at 149–150 when water is present. **Read More:** reflections change with surface movement and viewpoint; compare shape, value, and edge rather than flipping an object upside down. **Visual:** cd-core-l135-still-water.

### 136. Suggest simple moving water
**Practice (45 min):** Observe a stream or small river and identify its flow direction, surface-plane changes, bank or rock interruptions, and the way reflections break. Make a broad-shape version first; add a few directional marks where they explain movement, then compare and remove repetitive wave symbols. **Route:** Life water observation if available; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view moving-water reference. **Prereq/return:** 65–77, 126–127, 131–132, 135; return in 138 or a learner-chosen landscape at 149–150 when water is present. **Read More:** movement is suggested through observed direction and interruption, not a memorized water texture. **Visual:** cd-core-l136-moving-water.

### 137. Make quick outdoor sketches as conditions change
**Practice (45 min):** Produce a 3-minute thumbnail, a 5–10 minute visual note, and one short study from a single outdoor view or fixed reference. Choose a different purpose for each: major shapes, spatial layers, movement, value masses, or one distinctive relationship. If working from life, note the light state and shadow pattern early, establish large values, simplify wind-blown foliage, moving water, changing clouds, passers-by or cars, and omit changes that do not serve the drawing; compare with a supporting photo only when helpful. **Route:** Outdoor sit or observation is encouraged; fallback: ArtApp-owned references depicting changing light or moving forms. **Prereq/return:** 125–127, 132; optional feature studies 128–131 and 134–136; return in 138, 142, 146, 149–150. **Read More:** keep the drawing's original intention instead of chasing every change in weather, sunlight, people, or traffic. **Visual:** cd-core-l137-field-sketch.

### 138. Extended Study — compose and complete a landscape
**Practice (90 min):** Choose a manageable scene that combines one simple built form—such as a wall, bridge, fence, path, road, or small building—with two or three surrounding natural forms. Make several thumbnails, select a crop and focal area, organize major shapes and spatial layers, then complete a readable whole-scene drawing within the first 60 minutes: major shapes and depth, a limited value plan, and one comparison-led correction. Use the remaining 30 minutes to refine only selected edges/details, review the result, and stop deliberately. Apply terrain, tree, rock, foliage, and atmospheric studies as the view calls for them. If the scene contains water, bring the reflected-shape or flow decisions from 135–136 into the study; every landscape need not contain water. **Route:** Outdoor life drawing encouraged; fallback: ArtApp-owned fixed-view landscape reference showing a simple built form with natural forms and enough information for the selected study. **Prereq/return:** 125–127, 132–133; use 128–131 or 134–137 when the selected view calls for them; later transfer in 142–150. **Read More:** a substantial study combines earlier decisions; it does not need every landscape feature or a polished finish. **Visual:** cd-core-l138-landscape-study.

## Module 14 — Integrate and work independently (139–150)

**Source anchors:** D85 pp. 10–39, 148–171; P16 pp. 26–29, 81–104; GW14 pp. 116–159; N99 pp. 1–116.

### 139. Return to your first drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Redraw Lesson 01 subject from same view; compare saved baseline for placement, silhouette, proportion, and one relation; correct the new drawing and name what changed. **Route:** Life same object/view; fallback: saved reference and sketch. **Prereq/return:** 01, 15–19, 36–50; direct spaced retrieval, not score. **Read More:** compare attempts to choose a better next question. **Visual:** cd-core-l139-baseline.

### 140. Diagnose before you polish
**Practice (45 min):** Review one old drawing with a card: frame/focal read, whole shape, proportion, angle, gap, overlap, value, edge/detail. Step back, optionally mirror/flip, choose largest error, and correct same subject/view before trying new subject. **Route:** learner drawing or supplied pair; fallback: same-view reference. **Prereq/return:** 14, 22, 35, 48, 50, 64, 76, 88; return in 146–150. **Read More:** fresh subject tests transfer, not correction of the first drawing. **Visual:** cd-core-l140-diagnosis.

### 141. Audit a photograph before drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Compare a subject in life to its photo or two photos from different views; check crop, viewpoint, exposure, focus, omissions; choose evidence and draw one large relation. **Route:** Either; fallback: paired ArtApp-owned views. **Prereq/return:** 23, 51–64, 65–76; return in 145–150. **Read More:** photograph is a projection/record, not complete neutral ground truth. **Visual:** cd-core-l141-photo-audit.

### 142. Edit an arrangement — include, omit, emphasize
**Practice (45 min):** Thumbnail three crops of a real/supplied arrangement; decide what to omit, subordinate, or emphasize; compare focal read and draw one deliberate edit. **Route:** Either; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 23–35, 97, 119; return in 143, 146–150. **Read More:** selectivity can remain faithful to chosen intent. **Visual:** cd-core-l142-edit.

### 143. Simplify shape, form, value and edge
**Practice (45 min):** Make four 3-minute thumbnails of a complex object: largest shape, simplest volume, two/three value groups, selected edges/details. Compare them with the reference, choose one, and spend the remaining time developing and correcting that version. **Route:** Either cluttered tabletop or complex scene; fallback: clear ArtApp reference. **Prereq/return:** 06, 23–50, 65–97, 115–121; return in 144–150. **Read More:** simplification changes with the drawing question. **Visual:** cd-core-l143-simplification.

### 144. Turn a familiar object and rebuild it
**Practice (45 min):** Draw one small object, rotate slightly, draw again using axes/masses provisionally; compare silhouette, openings, overlaps and correct second view rather than copying the first. **Route:** Life; fallback: paired photos of same object. **Prereq/return:** 15–19, 36–50, 62–64; return in 146–150. **Read More:** a remembered construction must be checked anew. **Visual:** cd-core-l144-viewpoint-transfer.

### 145. Draw a room without relying on a diagram
**Practice (45 min):** Use placement, overlap, relative scale, floor contact, and intervals first; use perspective check only if it resolves an observed uncertainty; compare and correct the whole space. **Route:** Either learner room or supplied interior; fallback: same-view photo. **Prereq/return:** 51–64, 89–97, 141; return in 146–150. **Read More:** combine intuitive cues with formal perspective when useful. **Visual:** cd-core-l145-independent-space.

### 146. Choose the right approach in a small study
**Practice (45 min):** Select a structured subject; plan frame/big shapes, choose construction and graphite methods, draw, pause to compare/diagnose, correct same subject, decide where to stop. One main question; second optional. **Route:** Life or reference, object group/room/natural form; fallback: supplied image. **Prereq/return:** 23–35, 36–76, 89–97, 115–145; combines, little new theory. **Read More:** choose a method by subject and question. **Visual:** cd-core-l146-approach-study.

### 147. Extended Study — construct a full figure in space
**Practice (90 min):** Complete a full-body/three-quarter clothed figure in a setting, using a reference with one clearly foreshortened or overlapping limb. Plan composition/scale, apply gesture, support, proportion, landmarks, connected volumes, and hands/feet; compare and correct the same pose. **Route:** supplied matching figure/environment; fallback: compatible separate refs or self/mirror. **Prereq/return:** 20–21, 49, 51–64, 76–88, 106–114, 141–146; late form-to-anatomy transfer. **Read More:** sequence figure and setting without turning either into a diagram. **Visual:** cd-core-l147-figure-space.

### 148. Transfer likeness into clothing
**Practice (45 min):** Draw a half- or three-quarter crop of one specific clothed person; construct head and torso lightly, observe individual features and major fold directions, group values, then correct the largest likeness or structure error on the same view. Show hands/feet only if they fall within the chosen crop. **Route:** supplied person; fallback: self/mirror portrait and simple garment. **Prereq/return:** 78–88, 98–105, 122–124; use 106–114 only when the chosen crop includes hands or feet, and return to the diagnosis approach in 140. **Read More:** choose which error to correct first. **Visual:** cd-core-l148-likeness-drapery.

### 149. Plan an independent drawing
**Practice (45 min):** Choose an object, arrangement, room, person, portrait, hand/foot, natural form, or landscape and identify one main question. Decide life/reference, crop, composition, construction, graphite marks, and omissions; thumbnail and begin, revising the plan if observation changes the question. **Route:** learner choice; fallback: supplied ArtApp-owned reference. **Prereq/return:** use the diagnosis, editing, simplification and approach tools from 140, 142–143, 146; other subject studies are optional routes, not gates. **Read More:** planning is flexible, not a mechanical checklist. **Visual:** cd-core-l149-plan.

### 150. Extended Study — Independent Core Drawing — draw, diagnose, decide
**Practice (90 min):** Complete a learner-chosen life/reference drawing, with landscape as one of several valid subject families rather than a requirement; when chosen, water may return from 135–136 if it is visible. Plan the page, select methods, draw, step back, compare shape/space/value/focal read, correct one priority on the same subject, and stop deliberately. One main question is enough. **Route:** learner choice; fallback: same-view ArtApp-owned reference. **Prereq/return:** spiral of 01–149, not a mastery gate. **Read More:** record useful choices and one next question; completion is a beginning, not mastery. **Visual:** cd-core-l150-independent.
