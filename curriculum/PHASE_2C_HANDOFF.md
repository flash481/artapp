# Phase 2C handoff — lesson production

The approved architecture is [72 lessons](lesson-map.json) in six self-paced sections: Look and draw (01–12), Build form and light (13–24), Make space and design (25–36), Draw people and movement (37–48), Combine and interpret (49–60), and Choose and communicate (61–72). Read [docs/CURRICULUM.md](../docs/CURRICULUM.md), [docs/PEDAGOGY.md](../docs/PEDAGOGY.md), and [CURRICULUM_DECISIONS.md](CURRICULUM_DECISIONS.md) before drafting. The JSON map supplies each stable objective, exercise intent, warm-up direction, prerequisites, media, subject, visual need, source concept locator, and source exercise ID. The seven projects are 24, 36, 48, 59, 60, 68, and 72; lesson 71 is final-project planning.

1. Draft lessons in bounded batches, preserving map IDs and sequencing. Keep explanations brief, warm-ups relevant, main drawing substantial, and review questions concrete. Make the map's generic warm-up and optional-extension intents specific in the learner-facing lesson. Most lessons target about 45 minutes. Projects may use more time or multiple sessions.
2. Preserve the spiral: observation plus construction, measured checks, repeated value and composition, varied subjects, and decreasing scaffolding. Early lessons name the task; later ones leave subject, crop, lighting, focal point, medium, and interpretation increasingly to the learner. Prerequisites are support links, not mastery gates.
3. Use real objects and local views when feasible. Controlled references serve portrait, figure, difficult perspective, lighting, and selected landscapes. The flower commitment is deliberately small: required applications 04, 16, 34; flowers are optional in 66. Natural-form work elsewhere may use plants, leaves, shells, and other observed subjects. Landscape recurs from lesson 11 through the final section. Stylised character lessons are 12, 28, 53, 64 and apply fundamentals; a Pokémon reference is learner-provided for personal study only.
4. Graphite is primary through lesson 29. Introduce charcoal in 30 for broad value masses; revisit it in landscape, portrait, gesture, edges, and later choice. Colour painting is out of scope. Figure work emphasizes gesture, weight, major masses, useful landmarks, head form, and hand construction; avoid anatomy memorisation.
5. Visual strategy: original SVG for exact perspective, ellipse, form, or light diagrams; drawing from life for ordinary subjects; original/generated reference where controlled scenes matter; authentic verified public-domain artwork for the two master studies. `potentialSourceIllustrations` are private research leads, never deployable assets by default. Record provenance and distribution decisions under the asset standard.
6. Before the first real lesson files, version `curriculum/schemas/lesson.schema.json` so real lesson lifecycle statuses are possible while the three Phase 1 fixtures keep their exact warning. Do not substantially alter the app as part of this handoff.
7. Normal evidence path: **lesson map → `sources/research/QUICK_REFERENCE` → `sources/analyses/<book-id>.json` → targeted source page if needed**. The quick reference is selective; use the full `EXERCISE_INDEX.md` and `SELECTIVE_ILLUSTRATION_INDEX.md` for IDs absent from its shorter lists. Keep books, extracts, analyses, research, notes, and scratch material under private `sources/`. Never copy source illustrations or scans into the public app because of the password gate.

The [concept graph](concept-graph.json), [prerequisites](prerequisites.json), [coverage](coverage.json), [source mapping](source-mapping.json), and [exercise](exercise-audit.json), [subject](subject-audit.json), and [scaffolding](scaffolding-audit.json) audits are machine-readable review aids. Regenerate them from `node scripts/build-phase-2b.mjs` if the map changes, then recheck the human documents. No source illustration or master artwork has been cleared. Exact master-work selection and any specialist water or floral teaching claims require a targeted source/rights check at drafting time.
