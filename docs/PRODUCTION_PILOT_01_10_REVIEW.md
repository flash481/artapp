## Production system

The reusable entrypoint is [LESSON_PRODUCTION.md](LESSON_PRODUCTION.md), with a [canonical template](templates/CORE_DRAWING_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md), [visual specification](ARTAPP_VISUAL_SPEC.md), schemas and operational authoring, generation, independent lesson/image review, integration and curriculum-review skills. The pipeline separates checked private evidence, original lesson writing, visual contracts, creation, independent QA, integration, batch review, senior acceptance and engineering release checks. Production routing uses Luna Max for bounded bulk work and Sol 6.1 at medium reasoning for senior decisions.

Lessons follow the frozen sequence rather than legacy MVP numbering. Each has a recognizable subject, low-friction setup, owned fixed-view fallback, explicit comparison and correction, self-check, original optional Read More and checked citations. Warm-ups and illustrated mistakes are purposeful options. Timing budgets include looking, drawing, correction and five minutes of unassigned reserve. Preparation appears before the main teaching; essential actions are visible while sources and optional depth are collapsed. Canonical progress counts only canonical IDs while preserving historical completion, revisit and concept-practice data in storage and import/export.

## Evidence and provenance

Source checks, prompts, renders, rejected candidates and detailed review evidence stay under ignored `sources/production/`. Lesson authoring metadata and inline provenance are stripped from the public bundle. Lesson 09's exact submitted generation prompt became unavailable after a context interruption; its private record states this without inventing a reconstruction. Tool/date/output, approved text-only contract and rights route are retained. Future generation now saves the exact prompt before calling the tool and records output metadata immediately.

## Real-use questions

Agent QA does not establish learner effectiveness. Observe actual total time including setup and pauses, reading burden, warm-up value, phone visual usefulness, supplied-reference convenience, whether comparison leads to correction, satisfaction, erasing friction and optional Read More value. Pay particular attention to Lesson 03's pencil contact and Lesson 07's two crops in a 30-minute task. Findings can refine production without automatically reopening the architecture.
