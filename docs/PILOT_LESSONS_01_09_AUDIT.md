# Lessons 1–9 illustrated pilot: review record

This is the quality record for the nine published pilot lessons. Their approved order, titles, objectives, prerequisites, ordinary from-life subjects, timed drawing exercises, and approximately 45-minute sessions remain in place. Each lesson now has a concise core path, optional deeper reading, four original role-specific diagrams, diagnostic checks, and section-linked source citations. No book scan is used in the public app.

## Cross-lesson review

- **Pedagogy and progression:** Each lesson keeps one primary drawing decision. Looking and revising recur through contour, gap, proportion, line, framing, value, and construction tasks. The cup and bowl lessons use construction to propose a shape and observation to correct it.
- **Visual support:** Every lesson includes concept, warm-up, exercise, and mistake diagrams adjacent to the relevant instructions. The examples are schematic and deliberately unfinished; captions direct the learner back to a real subject. SVGs use text as well as colour to mark distinctions and can be enlarged in the app.
- **Workload:** The main exercise still occupies most of the approximately 45-minute session. **Go deeper**, reflection prompts, and **Sources & further reading** are collapsed by default; the self-check remains available without extra theory reading.
- **Source integrity and privacy:** Source locators were checked against the supplied books and used only for the ideas they support. Public diagrams are original SVGs with provenance records. No source scans or raw analysis are shipped; internal `bookReferences` and private `sourceImages` associations are stripped from the web bundle.

## Before scaling to Lessons 10–72

1. Run a real beginner usability session to see whether diagram labels and the ~45-minute pacing work beside a sketchbook. The current review checks rendering and content, not learner comprehension.
2. Keep verifying each later lesson against actual source pages. Digital editions without stable print pagination need clearly named digital locators, as in this pilot.
3. Use the private-source-image association only if an original diagram cannot teach the point. The current public static app has no authenticated image delivery, and no source-book image was selected for this pilot.
